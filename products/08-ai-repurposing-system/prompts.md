# Repurposing Prompts

Works with any AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). Paste your pillar content where it
says `[PASTE PILLAR]`, then run each prompt. Replace `[PRODUCT]` / `[LINK]`.

## Step 1 — Extract the core

> "Here's my pillar content: `[PASTE PILLAR]`. Extract: (a) the single big idea in
> one sentence, (b) the 5 key takeaways, (c) the best quotable line, and (d) the
> target reader. Keep it tight."

## Step 2 — Email

> "Using these takeaways, write a 150-word email in a friendly, conversational
> tone. Hook in the first line, deliver one takeaway, and end with a soft CTA to
> `[PRODUCT]`: `[LINK]`. Give me 3 subject line options too."

## Step 3 — X/Twitter thread

> "Turn the big idea and takeaways into a 6-tweet thread. Tweet 1 is a strong hook,
> tweets 2-5 deliver value (one idea each, under 280 chars), tweet 6 is a CTA to
> `[PRODUCT]`. No hashtags."

## Step 4 — LinkedIn post

> "Write a LinkedIn post (under 200 words) from the big idea. Start with a 1-line
> hook, use short single-sentence paragraphs, share a lesson, and end with a
> question to drive comments. Soft-mention `[PRODUCT]` at the end."

## Step 5 — Instagram/LinkedIn carousel

> "Create a 7-slide carousel from the takeaways. Slide 1 = hook title, slides 2-6 =
> one takeaway each (max 20 words), slide 7 = CTA to `[PRODUCT]`. Give me the
> on-slide text plus a caption."

## Step 6 — Short-video scripts (x3)

> "Write three 30-second short-video scripts (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) each based on a
> different takeaway. Each: a 3-second hook, the value, and a CTA to check my link
> for `[PRODUCT]`. Include suggested on-screen captions."

## Step 7 — Standalone captions (x5)

> "Write 5 standalone social captions from this content - one each in these tones:
> educational, story, contrarian, list, and urgent-promo (the promo one CTAs to
> `[PRODUCT]`)."

## Step 8 — Quote graphics text

> "Pull 5 punchy, quotable one-liners from this content I can put on quote graphics.
> Each under 12 words."

## Step 9 — Repurpose across formats (catch-all)

> "Take this `[blog post / video transcript]` and give me: 1 email, 1 X thread, 1
> LinkedIn post, 1 carousel outline, and 3 short-video hooks. Keep my CTA to
> `[PRODUCT]` natural, not salesy. Content: `[PASTE PILLAR]`."

## Step 10 — Next-week hook bank

> "Based on the topic of this content, generate 15 fresh hook lines I can use to
> create next week's posts for `[NICHE]`. Mix curiosity, benefit, and contrarian
> styles."

---

*10 prompts = one pillar into a full week of platform-native content.*
