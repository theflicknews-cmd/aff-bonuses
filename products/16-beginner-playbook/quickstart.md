# 7-Day Quickstart: Zero to Your First Promotion

A focused week to go from nothing to a live affiliate promotion. ~1-2 hours/day.

## Day 1 — Choose your lane
- [ ] Pick a niche using the 3-circle test (interest + demand + commissions)
- [ ] Decide your main platform (e.g., short-form video or a blog)
- [ ] Create/clean up your social profile or site

## Day 2 — Pick your offer
- [ ] Browse WarriorPlus / JVZoo / LaunchpadJV in your niche
- [ ] Shortlist 3 offers with good sales, reviews, and a funnel
- [ ] Score them (Product 20) and pick ONE
- [ ] Request affiliate approval + get your link

## Day 3 — Build your bonus stack
- [ ] Choose 3-5 bonuses from this vault that fit the offer
- [ ] Rebrand them (your name/links) and export to PDF
- [ ] Note each bonus's title + value

## Day 4 — Build your page
- [ ] Set up your bonus page (Product 2) with your link + bonus stack
- [ ] Add an honest affiliate disclosure
- [ ] Test the buy -> bonus delivery flow

## Day 5 — Set up capture & follow-up
- [ ] Create a lead magnet + opt-in (Product 6)
- [ ] Load the email swipe sequence (Product 4) into your autoresponder
- [ ] Connect opt-in -> thank-you -> bonus page

## Day 6 — Create content
- [ ] Write/record 3-5 pieces (Products 3, 7, 8, 15)
- [ ] Include CTAs to your opt-in / bonus page
- [ ] Schedule them across the next week

## Day 7 — Launch
- [ ] Publish your review/promo content
- [ ] Email your list (even if small)
- [ ] Post on every channel + pin the link
- [ ] Track clicks to your bonus page

## After day 7
Review what got clicks, double down on it, and repeat with the next offer. Your
second promo is always easier than your first.

---

*Done is better than perfect. Launch small, learn, and improve each round.*
