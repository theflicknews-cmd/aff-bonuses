## Chapter 1 — What affiliate marketing actually is

You promote someone else's product with a unique link. When someone buys through
your link, you earn a commission. No product creation, no inventory, no support.

The 4 players: **you (affiliate)**, the **vendor** (product owner), the **network**
(WarriorPlus/JVZoo/LaunchpadJV that tracks sales), and the **customer**.

## Chapter 2 — How you actually get paid

- **Commission %:** your cut per sale (often 50-100% on digital launches).
- **Cookies/tracking:** your link tags the buyer so you get credit.
- **Front-end + upsells:** you often earn on the whole funnel, not just the first sale.
- **Recurring:** subscriptions pay you every month.
- **Payouts:** the network pays you on a schedule (instant, weekly, net-30, etc.).

## Chapter 3 — Pick a niche (the 3-circle test)

Choose where these overlap: **interest** (you'll stick with it), **demand**
(people buy), **commissions** (worth promoting). Popular evergreen niches: make
money online, AI tools, software, health, relationships, hobbies.

## Chapter 4 — Find offers to promote

On WarriorPlus / JVZoo / LaunchpadJV, look for: high gravity/sales, good reviews,
strong funnel (upsells), reasonable refund rate, and vendor-provided assets.
Request approval, grab your link. (Use Product 20 to score offers.)

## Chapter 5 — The simplest funnel that works

```
Traffic -> Your page (review/bridge/opt-in) -> Vendor offer (your link) -> Bonus delivery
```

Never send cold traffic to a raw link. Capture emails when you can (Product 6).

## Chapter 6 — Get traffic

Free: short-form video, SEO, YouTube, communities, email (Product 9). Paid: ads
once you have a converting page (Product 13). Pick ONE free + build an email list.

## Chapter 7 — Add bonuses to win

When multiple affiliates promote the same offer, **bonuses decide who gets the
sale.** Stack 3-5 relevant bonuses (this entire vault) on a bonus page (Product 2).

## Chapter 8 — Stay legal & ethical

- Always disclose affiliate links (FTC + network rules).
- Only promote what you'd genuinely recommend.
- No fake scarcity, fake screenshots, or income guarantees.
- Follow each offer's promo rules.

## Chapter 9 — Scale

Double down on your best traffic source, build your email list, reinvest in ads,
and turn one-off promos into an evergreen review site (Product 11).
