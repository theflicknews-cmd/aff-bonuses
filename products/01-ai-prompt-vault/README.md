# Product 1 — AI Prompt Vault for Affiliate Marketers

**Niche:** AI + Affiliate Marketing
**Format:** Copy-paste prompt pack (export to PDF or deliver as a doc)
**Perceived value:** $47

A swipe-and-deploy collection of **60 battle-tested AI prompts** (works with
ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any LLM) that turn a blank page into affiliate
content fast. Every prompt has fill-in-the-blank `[brackets]` so buyers get a
result in seconds.

> **How to use:** Paste a prompt into your AI tool, replace the `[bracketed]`
> parts with your offer details, and run it. Chain prompts together for a full
> campaign.

---

## Section 1 — Product Research & Angle Finding

1. **Offer breakdown:** "Act as a direct-response copywriter. Here's a product
   description: `[PASTE]`. List the top 5 benefits, the core transformation it
   delivers, and the 3 biggest objections a buyer would have."

2. **Audience avatar:** "Create a detailed customer avatar for `[PRODUCT]`.
   Include demographics, daily frustrations, what they've already tried, and the
   exact words they'd use to describe their problem."

3. **Angle generator:** "Give me 10 unique marketing angles to promote
   `[PRODUCT]` to `[AUDIENCE]`. For each angle, write a one-line hook."

4. **Competitor gap:** "Here are 3 competing products: `[LIST]`. Identify what's
   missing from all of them that I can emphasize when I promote `[PRODUCT]`."

5. **Bonus-stack ideas:** "Suggest 7 bonus products I could create to complement
   `[PRODUCT]` and increase conversions for my affiliate promotion."

## Section 2 — Reviews & Content

6. **Honest review skeleton:** "Write a balanced affiliate review of `[PRODUCT]`
   for `[AUDIENCE]`. Include: what it is, who it's for, pros, cons, and a verdict.
   Keep it honest and conversational."

7. **Comparison post:** "Write a 'X vs Y' comparison between `[PRODUCT A]` and
   `[PRODUCT B]` with a summary table and a recommendation based on `[USE CASE]`."

8. **Listicle:** "Write a '7 best tools for `[GOAL]`' article and naturally
   position `[PRODUCT]` as the #1 pick. Include a short blurb per tool."

9. **FAQ section:** "Generate 10 frequently asked questions (with answers) that
   buyers ask before purchasing `[PRODUCT]`."

10. **Use-case stories:** "Write 3 short before/after scenarios showing how
    different people use `[PRODUCT]` to solve `[PROBLEM]`."

## Section 3 — Email Marketing

11. **Welcome sequence:** "Write a 3-email welcome sequence for a new subscriber
    interested in `[TOPIC]`, building toward a soft recommendation of `[PRODUCT]`."

12. **Launch sequence:** "Write a 5-email affiliate launch sequence for
    `[PRODUCT]` (open cart, value, social proof, objection-handling, last call).
    Keep each email under 200 words."

13. **Subject line bank:** "Give me 20 high-open-rate subject lines for an email
    promoting `[PRODUCT]`. Mix curiosity, benefit, and urgency styles."

14. **Re-engagement:** "Write a re-engagement email for subscribers who haven't
    opened in 30 days, leading into `[PRODUCT]`."

15. **PS-line swipes:** "Write 10 different PS lines I can add to a promo email
    for `[PRODUCT]`."

## Section 4 — Social Media

16. **Hook bank:** "Write 15 scroll-stopping first lines for social posts about
    `[TOPIC]` aimed at `[AUDIENCE]`."

17. **Carousel script:** "Create a 7-slide Instagram/LinkedIn carousel teaching
    `[MINI LESSON]` and ending with a CTA to `[PRODUCT]`."

18. **Short-video script:** "Write a 30-second TikTok/Reels/Shorts script with a
    hook, 3 value points, and a CTA for `[PRODUCT]`."

19. **Thread:** "Write a 6-tweet X/Twitter thread on `[TOPIC]` that builds
    authority and ends by recommending `[PRODUCT]`."

20. **Caption pack:** "Write 5 ready-to-post captions promoting `[PRODUCT]` in
    different tones: helpful, story-driven, contrarian, urgent, and casual."

## Section 5 — Bonus Page & Sales Copy

21. **Bonus page headline:** "Write 10 headlines for a bonus page where buyers
    of `[PRODUCT]` get my exclusive bonus stack: `[LIST BONUSES]`."

22. **Bullet benefits:** "Turn these features into 8 benefit-driven bullets:
    `[PASTE FEATURES]`."

23. **Objection crusher:** "List the 5 most likely reasons someone wouldn't buy
    `[PRODUCT]` and write a one-paragraph rebuttal for each."

24. **Guarantee/risk-reversal copy:** "Write 3 risk-reversal statements I can use
    to reassure buyers of `[PRODUCT]`."

25. **CTA variations:** "Give me 12 call-to-action button texts for promoting
    `[PRODUCT]`."

## Section 6 — Repurposing & Speed

26. **Repurpose engine:** "Turn this blog post into: 1 email, 3 tweets, 1 LinkedIn
    post, and 1 short-video script. Post: `[PASTE]`."

27. **Summarize for swipe:** "Summarize `[PRODUCT]`'s sales page into 5 bullets I
    can drop into my promo. Page: `[PASTE]`."

28. **Tone rewrite:** "Rewrite this in a `[friendly/expert/edgy]` tone: `[PASTE]`."

29. **Localize/personalize:** "Rewrite this promo for `[NICHE/AUDIENCE]`: `[PASTE]`."

30. **Headline A/B:** "Give me 5 A/B variations of this headline: `[PASTE]`."
