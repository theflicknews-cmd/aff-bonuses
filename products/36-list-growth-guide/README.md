# Product 36 — Email List Growth Guide

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing / List Building
**Format:** Growth strategy + 12 list-building tactics
**Perceived value:** $57

Your email list is the one asset you own (platforms can ban you overnight). This
guide shows buyers **12 proven tactics to grow an email list fast** and keep it
healthy — the single highest-ROI move in affiliate marketing.

## What's inside

- This guide — why the list wins + the 12 growth tactics
- [`tactics.md`](tactics.md) — each tactic with a step-by-step setup

## Why the list beats every other channel

- **You own it** — no algorithm, no ban risk to your reach.
- **Repeat promotions** — sell to the same people many times.
- **Highest conversions** — warm subscribers buy far more than cold traffic.
- **Compounds** — every promo can grow it further.

> A focused list of 1,000 engaged buyers beats 100,000 cold followers.

## The list-building flywheel

```
Traffic -> Lead magnet -> Opt-in -> Welcome sequence -> Value + offers
   ^                                                          |
   +---------------- referrals & repeat promos <--------------+
```

## Keeping the list healthy (deliverability)

- Welcome new subscribers immediately (sets the open habit).
- Email consistently with value (don't only show up to sell).
- Clean inactive subscribers periodically.
- Segment by interest/behavior for relevant offers.
- Avoid spam-trigger tactics; honor unsubscribes instantly.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Set up ONE strong lead magnet + opt-in (Products 6, 35).
2. Pick 2-3 traffic tactics and drive them to the opt-in.
3. Welcome + nurture with value (Product 4).
4. Promote relevant offers to a warm, segmented list.
