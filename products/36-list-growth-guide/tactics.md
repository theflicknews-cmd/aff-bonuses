# 12 List-Building Tactics (Step-by-Step)

Pick 2-3 and execute consistently. Replace `[FREEBIE]`, `[NICHE]`.

**1. Irresistible lead magnet**
- Solve one specific, urgent problem (Product 6).
- Make it fast to consume (cheat sheet, checklist, swipe file, quiz).
- Title it around the result, not the format.

**2. Dedicated opt-in page**
- One goal: the email. Remove navigation/distractions.
- Headline = the result; 3 bullets; one button (Product 6 copy).
- A/B test the headline.

**3. Quiz funnel**
- Build a "What's your `[type]`?" quiz (Product 35).
- Capture email before the result.
- Tag by result for targeted follow-up.

**4. Content upgrades**
- Add a downloadable bonus inside each blog post/video ("Get the checklist").
- Match the upgrade to the post topic for high opt-in rates.

**5. "Link in bio" hub**
- Route all social traffic to a hub linking to your opt-in (Products 15, 26).
- Lead with the freebie, not the offer.

**6. Lead-gen / opt-in ads**
- Run low-cost ads to your opt-in (Product 13).
- Optimize for cost-per-lead; retarget visitors who didn't opt in.

**7. Webinar registrations**
- Every registrant is a lead (Product 12).
- Promote the free training across channels.

**8. Giveaways & challenges**
- Run a "`[#]`-day `[NICHE]` challenge" with email signup to join.
- Giveaways grow fast but attract freebie-seekers — qualify with the prize.

**9. Guest content & collabs**
- Guest post / podcast / live with a CTA to your freebie (Product 9).
- Borrow other people's audiences.

**10. Pinterest / SEO to opt-in pages**
- Pin and rank content that links to a content upgrade (Products 18, 31).
- Evergreen, compounding opt-ins.

**11. Exit-intent / popups**
- Trigger a popup offering the freebie as visitors leave.
- Use timed or scroll-based popups on key pages.

**12. Referral / "share to unlock"**
- Reward subscribers for referring friends (extra resource, entry to a draw).
- Built-in growth loop.

---

## 30-day list sprint

- **Week 1:** Build lead magnet + opt-in page.
- **Week 2:** Add content upgrades + link-in-bio hub; start posting.
- **Week 3:** Launch a quiz or challenge; reach out for 2 collabs.
- **Week 4:** Add popups; review opt-in rate; double down on your best source.
