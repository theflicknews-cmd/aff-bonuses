## Custom assistant vs. one-off prompts

A one-off prompt solves one task once. A **custom assistant** has standing
instructions, your brand voice, and context baked in — so it produces on-brand
output instantly, every time, without re-explaining. It's the difference between
asking a stranger and having a trained teammate.

## How custom assistants work (any platform)

Most AI tools let you save a **system instruction** (Custom GPTs in ChatGPT, Projects
in Claude, "personas"/system prompts elsewhere). You define: its role, what it knows
about you/your niche, its rules, and its output format. Then you just give it the task.

## The 6 assistants every affiliate should build

1. **Content Writer** — drafts posts/blogs/emails in your voice.
2. **Copy Chief** — writes/critiques headlines, CTAs, sales copy (Products 17, 34).
3. **Research Analyst** — builds avatars + market research (Product 39).
4. **Repurposing Engine** — turns one pillar into many formats (Product 8).
5. **Offer Evaluator** — scores/analyzes affiliate offers (Product 20).
6. **Social Manager** — generates captions, hooks, and a posting plan (Products 3, 15).

## The anatomy of great assistant instructions

```
ROLE: who it is and its expertise
CONTEXT: your niche, audience, brand voice, and offers
RULES: do's and don'ts (tone, length, no hype, always add a CTA, etc.)
OUTPUT FORMAT: exactly how to structure responses
WORKFLOW: ask clarifying questions first, then deliver
```

## Tips for assistants that actually save time

- **Feed it your brand voice** — paste samples of your best writing.
- **Give it your offers** — so recommendations are always relevant.
- **Set output formats** — consistent structure = less editing.
- **Tell it to ask first** — clarifying questions beat wrong guesses.
- **Iterate the instructions** — refine as you spot weak outputs.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Pick your platform (ChatGPT Custom GPTs, Claude Projects, etc.).
2. Build the 6 assistants using the instruction templates.
3. Customize each with your niche, voice, and offers.
4. Use them daily; refine the instructions over time.
