## The universal video selling framework (HBPOC)

Every script in this pack follows the same proven flow:

1. **H — Hook** (first 3 seconds): stop the scroll / state the bold promise.
2. **B — Bridge**: agitate the problem, show you get it.
3. **P — Proof/Payoff**: teach or demo, prove it works.
4. **O — Offer**: introduce the product as the shortcut.
5. **C — CTA**: one clear next step (your link / bonus page).

> Rule of thumb: the shorter the video, the faster you hit the hook and CTA.
> Long-form earns the pitch; short-form front-loads it.

## Recording tips (for the affiliate)

- Read the hook twice as energetically as feels natural.
- Add on-screen captions (most watch muted).
- One CTA per video — don't split attention.
- Put the link in the description, pinned comment, AND say it out loud.
- Disclose your affiliate relationship (it builds trust and stays compliant).
