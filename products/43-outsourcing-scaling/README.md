# Product 43 — Outsourcing & Scaling Playbook

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing (advanced)
**Format:** Delegation system + hiring & SOP templates
**Perceived value:** $77

There are only so many hours in your day. To scale past a solo grind, you delegate.
This playbook shows buyers **what to outsource first, how to hire, and how to hand
off work** so their affiliate business grows beyond their own time.

## What's inside

- This guide — what to delegate + the hiring process
- [`delegation-templates.md`](delegation-templates.md) — role briefs + a hiring checklist

## When to start outsourcing

Outsource once a task is **repeatable, documented, and not the best use of your
time** — and you have income to reinvest. Start small (a few hours/week) and grow.

## What to outsource first (in order)

1. **Repetitive admin** — scheduling, formatting, uploading, data entry.
2. **Content production support** — editing video/graphics, repurposing (Product 8).
3. **Content creation** — drafting posts/blogs from your outlines (you approve).
4. **Engagement** — replying to comments/DMs from a script (Product 27).
5. **Specialized work** — SEO, ads management, design (as you scale).

Keep **strategy, key relationships, and your "voice"** for yourself (at least early).

## The hire-to-handoff process

```
1. Document the task (SOP) -> 2. Write a clear role brief -> 3. Source candidates
-> 4. Test with a small paid task -> 5. Onboard with the SOP -> 6. Review + refine
```

## Where to find help

- Freelance marketplaces (general VAs, designers, editors, writers).
- Niche communities/job boards for marketing VAs.
- Referrals from other affiliates/creators.
Start with project-based or part-time before committing to full-time.

## Writing SOPs (so handoff actually works)

An SOP = a simple step-by-step doc (or screen recording) of exactly how you do a
task. Rule: **if you've done it 3 times, document it.** Good SOPs make people
replaceable-proof your business and make training fast.

## Managing without micromanaging

- Give outcomes + standards, not just tasks ("publish 5 posts that match this style").
- Use a simple task board (Trello/Asana/Notion).
- Weekly check-in + async updates.
- Review quality against the SOP; refine the SOP, not just the person.

## The scaling mindset

You're moving from **doing the work** to **owning the system**. Your job becomes:
strategy, hiring, quality control, and growth. Pair this with automation (Product
19) — automate what you can, delegate what you can't.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. List your repetitive tasks; pick the first to delegate.
2. Write an SOP for it (`delegation-templates.md`).
3. Hire small + test before committing.
4. Onboard with the SOP, review, and refine. Repeat.
