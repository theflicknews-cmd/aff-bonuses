# Delegation Templates

## SOP template (use for every recurring task)

> **Task:** `[name]`
> **Goal / outcome:** `[what "done well" looks like]`
> **Frequency:** `[daily/weekly/per project]`
> **Tools needed:** `[list + logins location]`
> **Steps:**
> 1. `____________`
> 2. `____________`
> 3. `____________`
> **Quality standards:** `[checklist of what good output includes]`
> **Common mistakes to avoid:** `[list]`
> **Who to ask if stuck:** `[you / contact]`
> *(Attach a screen recording if helpful.)*

## Role brief template (for hiring)

> **Role:** `[e.g., Video Editing VA]`
> **About us:** `[1-2 lines about your brand/niche]`
> **What you'll do:** `[3-5 bullet tasks]`
> **Must-have skills:** `[list]`
> **Nice-to-have:** `[list]`
> **Hours/commitment:** `[part-time/project]`
> **Pay:** `[rate / per-project]`
> **How to apply:** `[instructions + a small filter task, e.g. "include the word
> BANANA so I know you read this"]`

## Test-task template (before you commit)

> Thanks for applying! Before we move forward, here's a small **paid** test task:
> `[specific, small version of the real work]`. Please complete it by `[date]` and
> include `[deliverable]`. This helps me see your work + communication style.

## Onboarding checklist

- [ ] Share the SOP for their first task
- [ ] Give access to required tools (use a password manager)
- [ ] Set up the task board (Trello/Asana/Notion)
- [ ] Agree on communication channel + check-in cadence
- [ ] Define "done" and quality standards
- [ ] Start with 1-2 tasks; expand as trust builds

- [ ] Monday: assign the week's tasks on the board
- [ ] Midweek: quick async check-in
- [ ] Friday: review output vs. standards; update SOPs
- [ ] Note what to delegate next
