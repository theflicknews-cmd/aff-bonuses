# Product 45 — Affiliate SOP & Systems Toolkit

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing (advanced)
**Format:** Standard operating procedures + business dashboards
**Perceived value:** $77

Hobbies are random; businesses run on systems. This toolkit turns affiliate
marketing into a **repeatable operation** with ready-made SOPs (standard operating
procedures) and dashboards — so buyers (and any future team) execute consistently
and nothing slips.

## What's inside

- This guide — the systems mindset + the core SOPs every affiliate needs
- [`sops.md`](sops.md) — copy-paste SOPs + daily/weekly/monthly dashboards

## Why systems beat motivation

Motivation is unreliable; systems are repeatable. When your key processes are
documented, you: execute faster, maintain quality, train help easily (Product 43),
and free your brain for strategy. **A documented business is a sellable, scalable asset.**

## The 7 core SOPs every affiliate needs

1. **Offer selection SOP** — how you vet and choose what to promote (Product 20).
2. **Promo launch SOP** — your repeatable promotion process (Product 29).
3. **Content production SOP** — idea -> create -> publish (Products 3, 8).
4. **Email SOP** — broadcast + sequence process (Products 4, 42).
5. **List growth SOP** — your repeatable lead-gen process (Product 36).
6. **Tracking & review SOP** — how/when you measure and decide (Product 24).
7. **Bonus delivery SOP** — how buyers reliably receive bonuses (Product 2).

## The 3 dashboards that run the business

- **Daily dashboard** — the few actions you do every day (post, engage, check stats).
- **Weekly dashboard** — content batched, emails sent, one growth experiment.
- **Monthly dashboard** — metrics review, what to scale/cut, next month's focus.

## How to build your operating system

1. For each recurring task, write a simple SOP (steps + standards).
2. Store them in one place (Notion/Google Drive) — your "company wiki."
3. Follow the daily/weekly/monthly dashboards.
4. Review and refine SOPs monthly (they're living documents).
5. Hand them to help when you scale (Product 43).

## The compounding payoff

Each documented system is a one-time investment that pays forever: less decision
fatigue, fewer dropped balls, faster execution, and a business that can grow beyond
your daily involvement.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Start with the SOP for your most frequent task (`sops.md`).
2. Adopt the daily/weekly/monthly dashboards.
3. Document one new SOP per week until your core 7 are covered.
4. Review monthly; delegate from the SOPs when ready.
