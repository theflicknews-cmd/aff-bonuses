# 30-Slide Webinar Deck

Format per slide: **on-slide text** + *(speaker notes)*. Replace `[brackets]`.

## Act 1 — Hook & Promise (slides 1-5, ~5 min)

**Slide 1 — Title**
`[Webinar Title]: How to [BIG RESULT] in [TIMEFRAME]`
*(Welcome people, tell them to grab a pen, close other tabs.)*

**Slide 2 — Who this is for**
`This is for [audience] who want [outcome] without [pain].`
*(Call out your exact avatar so they lean in.)*

**Slide 3 — The promise**
`By the end, you'll know exactly how to [specific promise].`
*(Make it specific and believable. This is the hook.)*

**Slide 4 — Why listen to me**
`[Your credibility / result / story in one line.]`
*(Brief - just enough authority. Don't brag.)*

**Slide 5 — What we'll cover**
`The 3 secrets to [result].`
*(Tease the 3 secrets. Promise to stay to the end for a special offer.)*

## Act 2 — Teach the 3 Secrets (slides 6-18, ~25 min)

**Slide 6 — Secret #1 title** `Secret 1: [Belief-shifting idea]`
**Slides 7-9 — Teach secret 1** *(Explain, give an example, make it actionable. Plant: "doing this manually is hard.")*

**Slide 10 — Secret #2 title** `Secret 2: [Idea]`
**Slides 11-13 — Teach secret 2** *(Same structure. Show a quick win.)*

**Slide 14 — Secret #3 title** `Secret 3: [Idea]`
**Slides 15-17 — Teach secret 3** *(The most important one - this is where the product naturally fits.)*

**Slide 18 — Recap** `So here's what you now know: [1, 2, 3].`
*(Summarize, then transition: "Now, you can do all this by hand... or shortcut it.")*

## Act 3 — Transition & Pitch (slides 19-27, ~15 min)

**Slide 19 — The gap** `The problem: doing this yourself takes [time/skill/money].`
**Slide 20 — Introduce offer** `That's why I recommend [PRODUCT].`
*(Position as the shortcut to everything you just taught.)*

**Slide 21 — What it is** `[Product] is a [category] that [core benefit].`
**Slide 22 — What you get (1)** `[Deliverable] -> [benefit]`
**Slide 23 — What you get (2)** `[Deliverable] -> [benefit]`
**Slide 24 — Value stack** `Total value: $[high anchor].`

**Slide 25 — Your bonuses** `Order through my link and also get: [BONUS 1, 2, 3].`
*(Your affiliate bonus stack - reference Products 1-11.)*

**Slide 26 — Price + urgency** `Today: just $[price]. Bonuses close [DEADLINE].`
**Slide 27 — Guarantee** `You're covered by [guarantee] - zero risk.`

## Act 4 — Close & Q&A (slides 28-30, ~10 min)

**Slide 28 — Clear CTA** `Go to [LINK] now and claim [PRODUCT] + bonuses.`
*(Walk them through clicking. Repeat the link out loud.)*

**Slide 29 — Recap offer** `Quick recap: [what they get + bonuses + guarantee].`

**Slide 30 — Q&A** `Your questions answered.`
*(Answer 5-8 common objections live. After each answer, repeat the CTA. End the
webinar on the offer slide, not a blank screen.)*
