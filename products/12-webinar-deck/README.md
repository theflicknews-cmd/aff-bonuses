# Product 12 — Webinar Slide Deck Template

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing / Webinars
**Format:** Slide-by-slide deck outline + speaker notes
**Perceived value:** $77

Webinars convert higher than almost any format because they teach, build trust,
and pitch in one sitting. This template gives buyers a **proven 30-slide webinar
structure** with speaker notes — fill it in, present (live or evergreen), and
promote any affiliate offer.

## What's inside

- This guide — the webinar conversion formula + timing
- [`slides.md`](slides.md) — all 30 slides with on-slide text + speaker notes

## The 4-act webinar formula

| Act | Slides | Minutes | Job |
|-----|--------|---------|-----|
| 1. Hook & promise | 1-5 | 5 | Earn attention, set the promise |
| 2. Teach (3 secrets) | 6-18 | 25 | Deliver real value, build authority |
| 3. Transition & pitch | 19-27 | 15 | Introduce the offer + bonuses |
| 4. Close & Q&A | 28-30 | 10 | Urgency, objections, CTA |

Total: ~45-55 minutes. The teaching earns the right to pitch; the pitch feels like
the natural next step, not a hard sell.

## Conversion levers built into the deck

- **The promise** (slide 3) — a specific, believable outcome.
- **The 3 secrets** (act 2) — each secret subtly reveals a gap the product fills.
- **The stack** (slides 22-24) — list everything they get, anchor the value high.
- **Bonuses + urgency** (slides 25-26) — your affiliate bonus stack with a deadline.
- **Risk reversal** (slide 27) — guarantee removes fear.
- **Q&A close** (slide 30) — answer objections live, repeat the CTA.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Fill in `slides.md` with your niche + the offer you promote.
2. Build slides in Google Slides/Canva/PowerPoint (one idea per slide).
3. Present live, or record once and run it evergreen.
4. Drive registrations with Product 10's webinar reg page.
5. Send the replay + last-call emails (Product 4).
