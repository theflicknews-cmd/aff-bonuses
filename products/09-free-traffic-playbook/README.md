**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing / Traffic
**Format:** 7 free traffic methods, step-by-step
**Perceived value:** $67

No ad budget? No problem. This playbook breaks down **7 free traffic sources** that
actually work for affiliate offers — with a step-by-step action plan for each, so
buyers can start driving clicks to their bonus page today.

## What's inside

- This guide — the 7 methods, each with steps, effort/speed rating, and pitfalls
- [`30-day-traffic-plan.md`](30-day-traffic-plan.md) — a day-by-day starter plan

## How to choose your traffic source

You don't need all 7. Pick **1 fast source + 1 compounding source**:

- **Fast (results in days):** Method 2 (Short-form video), Method 5 (Communities)
- **Compounding (results in weeks, then snowball):** Method 1 (SEO/Blog),
  Method 3 (YouTube), Method 6 (Email)

## The 7 free traffic methods

### Method 1 — SEO Blog Content
*Speed: Slow · Effort: Medium · Longevity: Very high*
1. Find buyer-intent keywords: "`[product]` review", "`[product]` vs", "best `[category]`".
2. Write a genuinely helpful review/comparison (use Product 1 prompts).
3. Add your affiliate link + bonus page link naturally.
4. Build 3-5 supporting posts that link to the main one.
**Pitfall:** thin, copy-paste content. Be the most useful result.

### Method 2 — Short-Form Video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)
*Speed: Fast · Effort: Medium · Longevity: Medium*
1. Post 1-3 short videos daily using Product 7 scripts.
2. Hook in 3 seconds; add captions.
3. CTA to "link in bio" (a link hub -> your bonus page).
4. Double down on whatever format gets views.
**Pitfall:** weak hooks. The first line is 80% of the result.

### Method 3 — YouTube
*Speed: Medium · Effort: Medium-High · Longevity: Very high*
1. Make review/tutorial/"X vs Y" videos targeting buyer keywords.
2. Affiliate link + bonus link in description and pinned comment.
3. Optimize title + thumbnail for clicks.
**Pitfall:** no clear CTA. Tell them exactly what to click and why.

### Method 4 — Pinterest
*Speed: Medium · Effort: Low-Medium · Longevity: High*
1. Create vertical pins linking to your blog post / bonus page.
2. Keyword-rich titles and descriptions.
3. Pin consistently to relevant boards.
**Pitfall:** linking straight to raw affiliate links (against rules) - link to your content.

### Method 5 — Communities & Forums
*Speed: Fast · Effort: Low · Longevity: Low-Medium*
1. Find where your audience hangs out (Reddit, FB groups, Discord, Quora).
2. Genuinely help - answer questions, no spam.
3. Link to your free lead magnet/content, not raw offers.
**Pitfall:** self-promo where it's banned. Read the rules; lead with value.

### Method 6 — Email List
*Speed: Fast (once built) · Effort: Medium · Longevity: Very high*
1. Drive traffic to a lead magnet (Product 6).
2. Nurture with value (Product 4 swipes).
3. Promote offers to a warm list - your highest-converting channel.
**Pitfall:** only emailing when you sell. Give 4x more than you ask.

### Method 7 — Strategic Collaborations
*Speed: Medium · Effort: Low · Longevity: Medium*
1. Find creators with a similar-size audience in `[NICHE]`.
2. Offer a value swap: shoutout, guest post, joint live, bundle.
3. Cross-promote each other's lists/offers.
**Pitfall:** asking before giving. Lead with what's in it for them.
