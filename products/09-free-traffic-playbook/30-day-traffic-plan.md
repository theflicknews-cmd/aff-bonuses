# 30-Day Free Traffic Starter Plan

A realistic plan combining one **fast** source (short-form video) and one
**compounding** source (a lead magnet + email list). Adjust to your niche.

## Week 1 — Set the foundation

- [ ] Day 1: Pick your offer + create your link hub / bonus page
- [ ] Day 2: Build a simple lead magnet (Product 6)
- [ ] Day 3: Set up opt-in page + autoresponder welcome email
- [ ] Day 4: Set up social profiles with a clear bio + link
- [ ] Day 5: Record/post your first 2 short videos (Product 7)
- [ ] Day 6: Answer 3 questions in a relevant community (value only)
- [ ] Day 7: Review — what got the most engagement?

## Week 2 — Build momentum

- [ ] Daily: 1-2 short videos (test different hooks)
- [ ] Day 8: Write 1 SEO blog post / YouTube review (buyer keyword)
- [ ] Day 10: Post your lead magnet in 2 communities (where allowed)
- [ ] Day 12: Email your new subscribers a value email
- [ ] Day 14: Review — which hook/topic drove the most clicks?

## Week 3 — Convert

- [ ] Daily: keep posting short video; reuse winning formats
- [ ] Day 15: First soft promo email to your list (Product 4)
- [ ] Day 17: Create a comparison post/video (high buyer intent)
- [ ] Day 19: Reach out to 3 potential collab partners
- [ ] Day 21: Review — opt-in rate and first clicks to offer

## Week 4 — Scale what works

- [ ] Daily: double down on your best traffic source
- [ ] Day 22: Repurpose your top post into 4 formats (Product 8)
- [ ] Day 24: Run a 3-day promo sequence to your list
- [ ] Day 26: Launch one collaboration / cross-promo
- [ ] Day 28: Update your best blog post / video for SEO
- [ ] Day 30: Full review — pick the ONE channel to go all-in on next month

---

## The one metric that matters early

Track **clicks to your bonus page**, not vanity likes. Traffic that doesn't click
doesn't earn. Once clicks are flowing, optimize the page (Product 2) to convert them.
