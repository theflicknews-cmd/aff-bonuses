## Reel hooks (pair with Product 15)
- "If you do `[NICHE]`, stop scrolling."
- "3 mistakes ruining your `[RESULT]`."
- "The tool I use to `[task]` in minutes."
- "Watch this before you `[action]`."

## Carousel template (7 slides)
- Slide 1: Hook title — "`[#]` ways to `[RESULT]`"
- Slides 2-6: one tip each (max 20 words + a visual)
- Slide 7: CTA — "Want the full `[FREEBIE]`? Link in bio 👇" (+ "Save this")
- Caption: expand on the hook, end with a question + CTA.

## Stories selling sequence (frames)
1. **Tease:** "Struggling with `[problem]`? 🧵" (poll: yes/no)
2. **Teach:** quick tip slide.
3. **Demo:** screen-record or photo of a result.
4. **Offer:** "I use `[PRODUCT]` for this. Grab it + my bonuses 👇" (link sticker).
5. **Urgency:** "Bonuses end `[deadline]` ⏳" (link sticker).
6. **Proof:** repost a DM/testimonial + link sticker.
