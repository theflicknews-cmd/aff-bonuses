# AI Market Research Prompts + Avatar Worksheet

Run these in any AI tool. Replace `[NICHE]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[AUDIENCE]`. Then validate
against real reviews/comments.

## Research prompts

**1. Build the avatar**
> "Act as a market researcher. Create a detailed customer avatar for `[AUDIENCE]`
> interested in `[NICHE]`. Include: demographics, psychographics, daily routine,
> goals, frustrations, what they've already tried, and where they hang out online."

**2. Pains**
> "List the top 10 pains, fears, and frustrations `[AUDIENCE]` have around `[NICHE]`.
> For each, give the underlying emotion."

**3. Desires**
> "List the top 10 desires and dream outcomes `[AUDIENCE]` want related to `[NICHE]`.
> Rank by how urgently they want each."

**4. Objections**
> "What are the 10 most common objections or hesitations `[AUDIENCE]` have before
> buying a product like `[PRODUCT]`? For each, suggest a reassurance."

**5. Their language (copy gold)**
> "Write 15 phrases `[AUDIENCE]` would actually say when describing their problem
> with `[NICHE]` - in their own casual words, not marketing speak."

**6. Buying triggers**
> "What events, emotions, or moments would trigger `[AUDIENCE]` to finally buy a
> solution like `[PRODUCT]`?"

**7. Hooks from the research**
> "Based on the pains and desires above, write 10 hooks/headlines that would grab
> `[AUDIENCE]`'s attention immediately."

**8. Objection-handling copy**
> "For each top objection above, write a one-paragraph rebuttal I can use in my
> emails and on my bonus page."

**9. Validate (what to look for)**
> "List the best places to validate this research for `[NICHE]` (specific review
> sites, subreddits, forums, social hashtags) and what to look for in each."

**10. Synthesize**
> "Summarize all of the above into a one-page 'message map': who they are, their #1
> pain, their #1 desire, their top objection, and the 3 phrases I should use most."
