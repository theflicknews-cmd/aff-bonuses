# Product 39 — AI Market Research & Customer Avatar Kit

**Niche:** AI + Affiliate Marketing
**Format:** Research prompts + avatar worksheet
**Perceived value:** $57

You can't sell to someone you don't understand. This kit uses AI to do **deep
market research and build a detailed customer avatar in minutes** — so buyers write
copy that hits, pick offers that fit, and target the right people.

## What's inside

- This guide — the research process + how to use the outputs
- [`research-prompts.md`](research-prompts.md) — AI prompts + an avatar worksheet

## Why research beats guessing

Most affiliates write copy based on what *they* think matters. Winners write based
on what the *market* actually wants, fears, and says. AI lets you simulate and
synthesize that research fast — then you validate it against real audience language.

## What you'll uncover

1. **The avatar** — who they are, day-to-day, demographics + psychographics.
2. **Pains** — the problems keeping them up at night.
3. **Desires** — the outcomes they crave.
4. **Objections** — why they hesitate to buy.
5. **Language** — the exact words/phrases they use (gold for copy).
6. **Buying triggers** — what makes them finally act.

## The research workflow

```
1. Describe your niche/offer to the AI
2. Generate the avatar + pains/desires/objections
3. Extract their language (phrases, fears, dreams)
4. Validate against real sources (reviews, comments, forums)
5. Feed it into your copy (Products 17, 34) and offer selection (Product 20)
```

> Validation matters: AI gives you a strong starting hypothesis. Confirm it by
> reading real reviews, social comments, and community posts in your niche.

## Where the research pays off

- **Copy** — use their exact words in headlines/emails (Products 17, 34).
- **Offer fit** — pick offers that match real pains (Product 20).
- **Lead magnets** — create freebies they actually want (Product 6).
- **Targeting** — better audiences for ads (Product 13).
- **Content** — topics that resonate (Products 3, 31).

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Run the research prompts for your niche/offer (`research-prompts.md`).
2. Fill the avatar worksheet with the outputs.
3. Validate against real reviews/comments.
4. Plug the language + pains into your copy and offers.
