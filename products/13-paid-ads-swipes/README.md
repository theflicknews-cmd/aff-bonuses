**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing / Paid Traffic

When you're ready to scale with paid traffic, blank-page syndrome kills momentum.
This swipe file gives buyers **20 fill-in-the-blank ad swipes** (Facebook/Meta,
Google, native, YouTube) plus targeting and budget guidance — so they can launch
profitable affiliate campaigns faster.

## What's inside

- This guide — ad frameworks, targeting, budget & scaling rules
- [`ad-swipes.md`](ad-swipes.md) — 20 ready-to-adapt ad swipes by platform

## The 3-part ad formula (every swipe uses it)

1. **Hook** — call out the audience or stop the scroll.
2. **Value/agitate** — the problem or the promise.
3. **CTA** — one clear action (-> your bridge/review/bonus page, *not* a raw
   affiliate link; most ad networks ban direct affiliate links).

> Compliance first: send paid traffic to YOUR page (bridge, review, or opt-in),
> never straight to the vendor. Avoid income guarantees and banned claims.

## Budget & scaling rules

- Start small: `[$10-20]`/day per ad set; one variable per test.
- Judge on **cost per lead / per click-to-offer**, not likes.
- Kill losers after enough data (e.g., spend = 1-2x target CPA, no conversion).
- Scale winners slowly (20-30%/day) or duplicate into new audiences.
- Always retarget page visitors who didn't buy (highest ROAS).

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Pick swipes matching your offer + platform.
2. Fill the `[brackets]`; match the angle to your page.
3. Launch 3-5 variations, let data pick the winner.
4. Point ads to your bridge/review/opt-in page (Products 10, 11, 6).
