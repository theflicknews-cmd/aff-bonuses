# Episode Templates & Scripts

Replace `[TOPIC]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[NICHE]`, `[LINK]`. Keep intros/outros consistent
for branding.

## Standard episode structure

1. **Cold open hook** (10-20s): the most interesting line first.
2. **Intro** (brand + what this episode covers).
3. **Main content** (teach/review/interview).
4. **Affiliate mention** (natural + show-notes CTA).
5. **Outro** (recap + subscribe + link CTA).

## Reusable intro script

> "Welcome to `[show name]`, the show where `[audience]` learn to `[result]`. I'm
> `[name]`. In today's episode: `[topic teaser]`. Let's get into it."

## Reusable outro script

> "That's it for today. If this helped, follow the show and share it with someone
> who needs it. Everything I mentioned - including `[PRODUCT]` and my free bonus
> stack - is linked in the show notes. See you next episode."

## Format 1 — Solo Teaching Episode

> **Hook:** "Here's the `[NICHE]` mistake costing you `[result]`..."
> **Teach:** 3 key points on `[TOPIC]`, one actionable tip each.
> **Affiliate:** "The tool that makes point #3 effortless is `[PRODUCT]` - link in
> the show notes, with a bonus if you grab it through me."
> **Outro.**

## Format 2 — Tool Review/Demo Episode

> **Hook:** "Is `[PRODUCT]` worth it? I've been using it for `[time]` - here's the
> honest verdict."
> **Body:** what it is, who it's for, what you like, what you don't, who should skip it.
> **Affiliate:** "If you want it, link + my bonuses in the show notes."
> **Outro.**

## Format 3 — Interview Episode

> **Hook:** a compelling quote from the guest.
> **Intro the guest** + why they're worth listening to.
> **Questions:** their story, their method, their best tip, a mistake to avoid.
> **Affiliate (soft):** if a relevant tool comes up, mention your link in notes.
> **Outro** + where to find the guest.

## Format 4 — Q&A Episode

> **Hook:** "You asked, I'm answering - including the big one about `[topic]`."
> **Answer 3-5 listener questions**, pointing to offers as solutions where relevant.
> **Affiliate:** "For the `[X]` question - the tool I recommend is `[PRODUCT]`,
> linked below."
> **Outro.**

## Format 5 — Case Study Episode

> **Hook:** "How `[person]` got `[result]` in `[time]`."
> **Break down:** the situation, the steps, the tools, the outcome, the lesson.
> **Affiliate:** "The key tool in this was `[PRODUCT]` - get it + bonuses via the
> show notes."
> **Outro.**

## 60-second spoken promo (mid/post-roll)

> "Quick note - if you're working on `[goal]`, the tool I personally use is
> `[PRODUCT]`. It `[benefit]`, and honestly it's saved me `[time/effort]`. If you
