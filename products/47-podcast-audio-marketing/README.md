# Product 47 — Podcast & Audio Marketing Guide

**Niche:** Social Media / Audio
**Format:** Audio content strategy + episode templates
**Perceived value:** $57

Audio is an underused affiliate channel with deep trust — listeners spend 20-40
minutes with your voice in their ears. This guide shows buyers how to use podcasts
and audio content (including short audio clips) to build authority and drive
affiliate sales.

## What's inside

- This guide — the audio opportunity + how to monetize with affiliate offers
- [`episode-templates.md`](episode-templates.md) — episode formats + scripts

## Why audio builds trust like nothing else

- **Intimacy:** your voice in their ears for long stretches = strong connection.
- **Low competition:** fewer creators than video/text in most niches.
- **Multitaskable:** people listen while driving, walking, working.
- **Repurposable:** one episode becomes clips, quotes, a blog post, and emails.

## Two ways to use audio (you don't need a full podcast)

1. **A real podcast** — recurring episodes on Spotify/Apple/etc. Builds a loyal
   audience over time.
2. **Audio content / clips** — short audio on social (audiograms, voice posts,
   X Spaces, IG/FB audio). Faster to start, great for reach.

## Episode formats that fit affiliate marketing

| Format | What it is | Affiliate fit |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Solo teaching | You teach one topic | Recommend a tool at the end |
| Tool review/demo | Talk through a product | Direct affiliate promo |
| Interview | Guest expert/creator | Soft mentions + collab |
| Q&A | Answer listener questions | Point to offers as solutions |
| Case study | Break down a result | The tool/method = your offer |

## How to monetize audio with affiliate offers

1. **Mention naturally** — "the tool I use for this is `[PRODUCT]`."
2. **Dedicated CTA** — "links + my bonus stack in the show notes."
3. **Show notes** — your affiliate + bonus links (Product 2).
4. **A spoken promo segment** — a 60-second mid/post-roll for an offer.
5. **Drive to your list** — "grab the free `[lead magnet]` at `[link]`."

> Disclose affiliate relationships verbally and in show notes (Product 28).

## Getting started (lean setup)

- A decent USB mic + free recording/editing software.
- Pick a format + cadence you can sustain (weekly/bi-weekly).
- Write episode outlines (use Product 1 prompts).
- Publish + repurpose into clips (Product 8).

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Choose: full podcast or audio clips to start.
2. Pick a format and use the episode templates (`episode-templates.md`).
3. Add affiliate + bonus links to every show note.
4. Repurpose each episode into social clips + an email.
