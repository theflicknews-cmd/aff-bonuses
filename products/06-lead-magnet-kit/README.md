# Product 6 — Lead Magnet Creation Kit

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing / List Building
**Format:** 10 done-for-you lead magnet blueprints + opt-in copy
**Perceived value:** $67

The list is the asset. This kit hands buyers **10 plug-and-play lead magnet
blueprints** that attract the *right* subscribers (buyers, not freebie-seekers)
and pre-sell them for any affiliate offer — plus opt-in page copy that converts.

## What's inside

- This guide — 10 lead-magnet blueprints with structure & angle
- [`optin-copy.md`](optin-copy.md) — fill-in-the-blank opt-in page + thank-you copy

## Why most lead magnets fail

They attract freebie-seekers, deliver no quick win, and never connect to an offer.
A great lead magnet is: **specific, fast to consume, and a bridge to the paid
solution**. Each blueprint below is built that way.

---

## The 10 lead-magnet blueprints

**1. The Cheat Sheet**
One page, scannable, "everything you need on `[TOPIC]` in one place." Fastest to
make, highest perceived utility. Bridge: "The tool that automates this -> `[OFFER]`."

**2. The Checklist**
Step-by-step list for `[OUTCOME]`. Great for processes (launch, setup, audit).
Bridge: position `[OFFER]` as the step that's hardest to do manually.

**3. The Swipe File**
"`[#]` proven `[emails/headlines/scripts]` you can copy." High value, low effort
to consume. Bridge: "Want these auto-generated? -> `[AI OFFER]`."

**4. The Template / Toolkit**
A fill-in-the-blank doc, spreadsheet, or Canva template. Bridge: the offer that
makes the template work faster.

**5. The Mini-Course (email)**
A 5-day email course teaching one skill. Builds the email relationship and warms
toward the offer on day 4-5.

**6. The Resource List**
"`[#]` best tools/resources for `[GOAL]`." Naturally features your affiliate
offer as the top pick.

**7. The Quiz / Assessment**
"What's your `[X]` score?" Interactive, high opt-in rates, segments your list.
Bridge: the result page recommends `[OFFER]` based on their answer.

**8. The Calculator / Worksheet**
A simple tool that gives a personalized number (ROI, savings, time). Bridge:
"Improve your number with `[OFFER]`."

**9. The Case Study / Report**
"How `[PERSON]` got `[RESULT]` in `[TIME]`." Story-driven proof. Bridge: the exact
tool/method used = your offer.

**10. The Mini-Ebook / Guide**
A short, focused PDF on solving one problem. Bridge: a CTA section and PS pointing
to the offer.

## Pick-your-magnet matrix

| If your audience wants... | Use blueprint |
|---------------------------|---------------|
| A quick answer | Cheat Sheet (1) |
| To follow a process | Checklist (2) |
| Done-for-you assets | Swipe File (3) / Template (4) |
| To learn over time | Mini-Course (5) |
| Tool recommendations | Resource List (6) |
| Personalization | Quiz (7) / Calculator (8) |
| Proof it works | Case Study (9) |
| Depth on one topic | Mini-Ebook (10) |
