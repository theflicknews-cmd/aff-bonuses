# Product 38 — Personal Brand & Authority Building Guide

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing / Branding
**Format:** Brand-building strategy + positioning worksheet
**Perceived value:** $67

People buy from people they know, like, and trust. A personal brand turns you from
"random affiliate link" into a **trusted recommendation** — which means higher
conversions on every promo. This guide shows buyers how to build authority in their
niche.

## What's inside

- This guide — the authority-building system + content pillars
- [`positioning-worksheet.md`](positioning-worksheet.md) — define your brand fast

## Why a personal brand multiplies affiliate income

- **Trust = conversions** — your recommendation carries weight.
- **You stand out** — when 50 affiliates push the same offer, people buy from *you*.
- **Premium positioning** — you can promote higher-ticket offers (Product 23).
- **Durability** — your audience follows *you*, not a single platform or offer.

## The 4 pillars of authority

1. **Clarity** — who you help + the result you help them get (one sentence).
2. **Consistency** — show up regularly with a recognizable voice + look.
3. **Credibility** — proof: your results, client wins, knowledge, transparency.
4. **Connection** — be human: share story, personality, and genuine interaction.

## Your content pillars (the "authority mix")

Pick 3-5 themes you'll be known for. For each, you create value content that builds
trust and sets up offers:

- **Teach** — how-tos and tips in your niche (the bulk of your content).
- **Prove** — results, case studies, behind-the-scenes.
- **Story** — your journey, lessons, personality.
- **Curate** — tools, resources, recommendations (where offers fit naturally).
- **Engage** — questions, opinions, community.

## Building authority without "being an expert" yet

You don't need to be the #1 expert — just **a few steps ahead** of your audience and
**transparent** about your journey. "Documenting" (sharing what you're learning/doing)
builds trust faster than pretending to know everything.

## Consistency system (so the brand actually compounds)

- Pick ONE primary platform to go deep on.
- Show up on a predictable schedule (Product 3 calendar).
- Reuse one pillar piece across formats (Product 8).
- Always capture emails so the relationship deepens (Products 6, 36).

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Complete the positioning worksheet (`positioning-worksheet.md`).
2. Pick 3-5 content pillars + one primary platform.
3. Show up consistently with the authority mix.
4. Let trust do the selling on your promos.
