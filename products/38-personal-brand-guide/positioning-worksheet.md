# Brand Positioning Worksheet

Fill this in once. It becomes the foundation of your bio, content, and offers.

## 1. Who you help (your audience)
- My audience is: `________________`
- They want: `________________`
- They struggle with: `________________`
- In their words, their problem sounds like: `________________`

## 2. The result you help them get
- I help `[audience]` achieve `[result]` without `[pain]`.
- One-sentence positioning: `________________`

## 3. Your unique angle (why you)
- My background/story that relates: `________________`
- What I do differently from others in `[niche]`: `________________`
- My "few steps ahead" advantage: `________________`

## 4. Your content pillars (pick 3-5)
- [ ] Teach: topics `________________`
- [ ] Prove: results/proof I can share `________________`
- [ ] Story: experiences I can tell `________________`
- [ ] Curate: tools/offers I recommend `________________`
- [ ] Engage: opinions/questions `________________`

## 5. Your brand voice
- 3 words that describe my voice: `____ / ____ / ____`
- Tone: `friendly / expert / edgy / motivational / ____`
- Things I'll always do / never do: `________________`

## 6. Visual identity (keep it simple + consistent)
- Colors: `________________`
- Profile photo style: `________________`
- Recurring template/look for graphics: `________________`

## 7. Your platform + cadence
- Primary platform: `________________`
- Posting cadence: `________________`
- Email cadence: `________________`

## 8. Your bio (assemble from the above)
> `[Name]` - I help `[audience]` `[result]` without `[pain]`.
> `[Proof/credibility line]`. Free `[freebie]` 👇 `[link]`

## 9. Proof bank (collect over time)
- Results/numbers: `________________`
- Testimonials/DMs: `________________`
- Milestones: `________________`

---

## Your positioning statement (final)

> I help **`[audience]`** to **`[result]`** by **`[your method/angle]`**, so they can
> **`[bigger outcome]`** - without **`[main pain]`**.

Use this everywhere: bio, content intros, opt-in pages, and pitches.

---

*Clarity first. When you know exactly who you help and how, every piece of content
and every promo gets easier - and more convincing.*
