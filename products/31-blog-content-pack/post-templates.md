# Blog Post Templates + SEO Checklist

Fill the `[brackets]`. Each outline is SEO-structured. Draft fast with Product 1's prompts.

---

## Template 1 — Product Review
- **H1:** `[Product]` Review (2026): Honest Pros, Cons & Verdict
- Intro: who it's for + your verdict in 2 lines
- What is `[Product]`?
- Who it's for / not for
- Key features (H2s) with benefits
- Pricing & plans
- Pros and cons
- My exclusive bonuses (-> bonus page)
- Alternatives (internal links)
- FAQ (5-7 Qs)
- Verdict + CTA + disclosure

## Template 2 — "Best Of" Listicle
- **H1:** `[#]` Best `[Category]` for `[Audience]` (2026)
- Intro + how you chose
- Comparison table (name/best for/price/CTA)
- #1 pick (most detail) ... #`[#]` pick
- Buyer's guide: how to choose
- FAQ
- Verdict + CTA + disclosure

## Template 3 — Comparison
- **H1:** `[A]` vs `[B]`: Which Is Better for `[use case]`? (2026)
- Quick answer up top
- Side-by-side table
- `[A]` overview / `[B]` overview
- Head-to-head (price, features, ease, support)
- Which to pick by scenario
- CTA + disclosure

## Template 4 — Alternatives
- **H1:** `[#]` Best `[Product]` Alternatives in 2026
- Why look for alternatives
- Each alternative: best-for, pros/cons, CTA
- Comparison table
- Top recommendation + CTA + disclosure

## Template 5 — How-To / Tutorial
- **H1:** How to `[Result]` in `[#]` Steps (2026 Guide)
- Intro: the outcome + who it's for
- Step-by-step H2s
- Tools you'll need (link your offer naturally)
- Common mistakes
- FAQ
- CTA to bonus/offer + disclosure

## Template 6 — Resource Roundup
- **H1:** `[#]` Best Tools & Resources for `[Goal]`
- Intro
- Categories of tools, each with a short blurb + CTA
- Your #1 recommended pick (your offer)
- CTA + disclosure

---

## On-Page SEO Checklist (run on every post)

- [ ] Primary keyword in: title, URL slug, first 100 words, one H2
- [ ] Title under ~60 chars; meta description under ~155 chars
- [ ] Content fully answers the search intent (better than what ranks)
- [ ] Original insight/opinion (not generic AI filler)
- [ ] 2-5 internal links (info -> review -> "best of")
- [ ] 1-2 relevant external links (credibility)
- [ ] Images with descriptive alt text
- [ ] Clear CTA to bonus page + email opt-in
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure near the top
- [ ] Mobile-friendly + fast loading
- [ ] Scannable: short paragraphs, H2/H3s, bullets, a table
- [ ] FAQ section (targets "People also ask")
