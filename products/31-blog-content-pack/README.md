## The keyword intent ladder (write for buyers)

| Intent | Example keyword | Converts? |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|
| Transactional | "buy `[product]`", "`[product]` discount" | 🔥 Highest |
| Commercial | "`[product]` review", "best `[category]`", "`[A]` vs `[B]`" | 🔥 High |
| Informational | "how to `[task]`", "what is `[topic]`" | Medium (build trust + internal-link to money pages) |

Prioritize commercial-intent keywords first — they have buyers; then build
informational posts that link to them for authority.

## Simple keyword research (no paid tools needed)

1. Type "`[product]`" and "best `[category]`" into Google/YouTube; note autocomplete.
2. Check "People also ask" and "Related searches."
3. Look at what's ranking — can you make something more useful?
4. Target long-tail ("best `[category]` for `[specific audience]`") — easier to rank.

## The 6 blog post types that earn (templates in `post-templates.md`)

1. **Product review** — "`[product]` review"
2. **"Best of" listicle** — "best `[category]` for `[goal]`"
3. **Comparison** — "`[A]` vs `[B]`"
4. **Alternatives** — "best `[product]` alternatives"
5. **How-to / tutorial** — builds trust, links to money pages
6. **Resource/roundup** — "tools/resources for `[goal]`"

## On-page SEO essentials (full checklist inside)

- Keyword in title, URL, first 100 words, and one H2.
- Helpful, original content that fully answers the query.
- Internal links: info posts -> reviews -> "best of" pillars.
- Clear CTAs to your bonus page + email opt-in.
- Fast, mobile-friendly page; descriptive image alt text.
