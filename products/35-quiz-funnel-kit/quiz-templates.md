# Quiz Funnel Templates

Replace `[NICHE]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[RESULT]`, `[TYPE]`. Build in any quiz/funnel tool.

## Quiz title templates (the hook)

- What's Your `[NICHE]` Type? (60-Second Quiz)
- Which `[Product/Strategy]` Is Right for You?
- How `[Ready/Optimized]` Is Your `[X]`? Take the Quiz
- Find Your Ideal `[NICHE/Path]` in 60 Seconds
- What's Holding Back Your `[RESULT]`? Quiz Inside

## Landing copy

> **`[Quiz Title]`**
> Answer `[#]` quick questions and get your personalized `[result type]` - plus a
> tailored recommendation to help you `[benefit]`. Takes 60 seconds.
> **[Start the Quiz]**

## Question templates (write 3-7)

**Q1 — Current situation**
> Where are you right now with `[NICHE]`?
> a) Just starting  b) Some progress  c) Stuck/plateaued  d) Scaling

**Q2 — Goal**
> What's your main goal?
> a) `[goal A]`  b) `[goal B]`  c) `[goal C]`  d) `[goal D]`

**Q3 — Biggest obstacle**
> What's your biggest challenge?
> a) `[obstacle A]`  b) `[obstacle B]`  c) `[obstacle C]`  d) `[obstacle D]`

**Q4 — Preference/style**
> How do you prefer to work?
> a) `[style A]`  b) `[style B]`  c) `[style C]`

**Q5 — Resources**
> What do you have to work with?
> a) Time, little money  b) Money, little time  c) Both  d) Neither yet

*(Map answers to result buckets - e.g., mostly A = "Beginner Builder" type.)*

## Email capture (before result)

> 🎉 Your result is ready! Enter your email and we'll send your `[result type]`
> plus your personalized `[NICHE]` action steps.
> **[See My Result]**

## Result page template (one per result bucket)

> **You're a `[TYPE]`!**
> `[2-3 sentences affirming their result and what it means.]`
>
> **Your next step:** Based on your answers, the best fit for you is `[PRODUCT]` -
> it helps `[TYPE]`s like you `[benefit]`.
> 👉 **[Get `[PRODUCT]` + My Bonuses]** (`[link to bonus page]`)
>
> *(Also delivered to your inbox.)*

## Follow-up sequence (tag by result)

- **Email 1:** Deliver result + one quick tip for their `[TYPE]`.
- **Email 2:** Teach + show why `[PRODUCT]` fits their result.
- **Email 3:** Proof/story from someone with the same result.
- **Email 4:** Offer + bonus + soft urgency (use Product 4 swipes).

## Example: "What's Your Affiliate Marketer Type?"
- Results: The Content Creator, The Email Marketer, The Paid-Traffic Scaler, The
  Community Builder.
- Each result recommends the matching tool/offer + relevant vault bonuses.

---

*A quiz turns a cold visitor into a segmented, warmed-up lead who gets a
recommendation they actually asked for. Highest-converting opt-in you can run.*
