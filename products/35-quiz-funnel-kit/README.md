# Product 35 — Quiz Funnel Kit

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing / Lead Generation
**Format:** Quiz funnel blueprint + question templates
**Perceived value:** $57

Quizzes are one of the highest-converting lead magnets that exist — they're
interactive, fun, and give people a personalized result they *want*. This kit shows
buyers how to build a quiz funnel that captures leads and routes them to the right
affiliate offer.

## What's inside

- This guide — how quiz funnels work + the conversion flow
- [`quiz-templates.md`](quiz-templates.md) — quiz structures + question/result templates

## Why quiz funnels convert so well

- **Interactive** — higher engagement than a static opt-in.
- **Personalized** — people love results about themselves.
- **Segmentation** — answers tell you exactly what to offer each person.
- **Curiosity** — "What's your `[X]` type?" pulls the click.
- **Natural recommendation** — the result page recommends the *right* offer.

## The quiz funnel flow

```
Quiz title/landing -> Questions (3-7) -> Email capture -> Personalized result
   -> Tailored offer recommendation (your affiliate link / bonus page)
   -> Follow-up email sequence based on their result
```

## The 4 quiz types that work for affiliates

1. **"What type are you?"** — personality/style quiz (e.g., "What's your affiliate
   marketer type?"). Result -> matching offer.
2. **"What should you `[do/choose]`?"** — recommendation quiz (e.g., "Which `[tool]`
   is right for you?"). Result -> recommended product (your link).
3. **"Score/assessment"** — "How `[ready/optimized]` is your `[X]`?" Result reveals
   gaps your offer fills.
4. **"Find your `[X]`"** — diagnostic (e.g., "Find your ideal `[niche/strategy]`").

## Designing a high-converting quiz

- **Title:** curiosity + benefit ("What's Your `[X]` Type? (60-Second Quiz)").
- **3-7 questions** — short enough to finish, long enough to personalize.
- **Capture email before the result** ("Enter your email to see your result").
- **Result page:** affirm their result + recommend the matching offer + bonus.
- **Follow-up:** tag by result and send a tailored email sequence (Product 4).

## Tools to build it

Most quiz/funnel builders work (Interact, Typeform, ScoreApp, or your page builder).
You only need: questions, email capture, result logic, and a redirect/recommendation.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Pick a quiz type that ends in your offer being the natural recommendation.
2. Use the templates (`quiz-templates.md`) to write questions + results.
3. Add email capture before the result.
4. Route each result to the right offer/bonus page; tag for follow-up.
