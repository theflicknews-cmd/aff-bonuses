# Product 30 — The 90-Day Affiliate Action Plan

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing
**Format:** 90-day roadmap (capstone)
**Perceived value:** $97

The capstone of the vault. Most people fail at affiliate marketing because they
have *tactics* but no *plan*. This 90-day roadmap turns everything in this vault
into a clear, week-by-week path — from setup to a repeatable, scaling system.

## What's inside

- This guide — the 90-day overview + monthly objectives
- [`weekly-plan.md`](weekly-plan.md) — the week-by-week checklist (all 12 weeks)

## The 90-day philosophy

- **Month 1 — Foundation:** pick your lane, set up assets, launch your first promo.
- **Month 2 — Traffic & List:** drive consistent traffic, build your email list.
- **Month 3 — Optimize & Scale:** double down on winners, add a launch, systemize.

You won't do everything — you'll do the *right* things in the right order. Consistency
over 90 days beats intensity for a weekend.

## Month 1 — Foundation (weeks 1-4)

**Objective:** Be live with your first promotion and first subscribers.
- Pick niche + offer (Products 16, 20)
- Build bonus page + stack (Products 2, 25)
- Set up lead magnet + opt-in + email sequence (Products 6, 4)
- Create your first content (Products 3, 7, 17)
- Launch your first promo

**Month 1 win:** first clicks, first opt-ins, ideally first commission.

## Month 2 — Traffic & List (weeks 5-8)

**Objective:** Consistent traffic from one main channel + a growing list.
- Commit to ONE primary traffic source (Products 9, 15, 18, 21, 22, 26)
- Publish consistently; build the content habit (Product 8 to produce fast)
- Grow and nurture your email list (Product 4)
- Start a review/comparison asset (Products 11, 7)

**Month 2 win:** repeatable daily/weekly traffic + a list you can promote to.

## Month 3 — Optimize & Scale (weeks 9-12)

**Objective:** A profitable, repeatable system you can scale.
- Track metrics; fix your weakest funnel step (Product 24)
- Run a full launch promotion (Product 29)
- Add a second traffic source or automate part of the workflow (Product 19)
- Consider paid traffic on a proven funnel (Product 13)
- Systemize what works into a repeatable playbook

**Month 3 win:** you know your EPC, have a repeatable promo system, and a plan to scale.

## The 4 rules for the 90 days

1. **One thing at a time:** master one traffic source before adding another.
2. **Ship daily:** done beats perfect; consistency compounds.
3. **Capture emails always:** own your audience.
4. **Measure & adjust:** let data, not feelings, drive decisions.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Block 1-2 hours/day for 90 days.
2. Follow the weekly checklist (`weekly-plan.md`) in order.
3. Don't skip ahead — each week builds on the last.
4. Review at the end of each month; adjust and continue.
