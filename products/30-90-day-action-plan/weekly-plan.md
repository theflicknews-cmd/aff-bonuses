# The 12-Week Action Plan

~1-2 hours/day. Check off each task. Product numbers reference the vault.

## MONTH 1 — FOUNDATION

### Week 1 — Choose your lane
- [ ] Pick a niche (Product 16, 20)
- [ ] Pick ONE primary traffic source you'll commit to
- [ ] Shortlist + score 3 offers; choose one (Product 20)
- [ ] Get affiliate approval + your link
- [ ] Set up/clean your platform or site

### Week 2 — Build your assets
- [ ] Choose 3-5 bonuses from the vault (Product 25)
- [ ] Rebrand them + export PDFs
- [ ] Build your bonus page (Product 2)
- [ ] Create a lead magnet (Product 6)
- [ ] Set up opt-in + thank-you page

### Week 3 — Set up follow-up + content
- [ ] Load the email sequence (Product 4) into your autoresponder
- [ ] Add disclosures everywhere (Product 28)
- [ ] Create your first 5 pieces of content (Products 3, 7, 17)
- [ ] Schedule the content

### Week 4 — First promo
- [ ] Publish your review/promo content
- [ ] Email your list (however small)
- [ ] Post on every channel; pin your link
- [ ] Track clicks + opt-ins (Product 24)
- [ ] Review: what worked? what didn't?

## MONTH 2 — TRAFFIC & LIST

### Week 5 — Commit to one traffic source
- [ ] Pick your channel (Products 9, 15, 18, 21, 22, 26)
- [ ] Set a realistic posting cadence
- [ ] Batch-create a week of content (Product 8)

### Week 6 — Build the habit
- [ ] Post consistently every day/scheduled
- [ ] Engage with your audience daily
- [ ] Note your best-performing hooks/topics

### Week 7 — Grow & nurture the list
- [ ] Drive traffic to your lead magnet
- [ ] Send weekly value emails (Product 4)
- [ ] Start a review or comparison asset (Products 11, 7)

### Week 8 — Double down
- [ ] Make more of what's working (reuse winning hooks)
- [ ] Publish your review/comparison content
- [ ] Review month 2 metrics (Product 24)

## MONTH 3 — OPTIMIZE & SCALE

### Week 9 — Optimize the funnel
- [ ] Map your full funnel + find the weakest step (Product 24)
- [ ] Change ONE variable to fix it
- [ ] Re-measure and compare

### Week 10 — Run a launch
- [ ] Find a launch with a leaderboard (Product 29)
- [ ] Plan the launch timeline + bonus stack
- [ ] Pre-warm your audience

### Week 11 — Execute + expand
- [ ] Run the launch (open hard, close hard)
- [ ] Debrief the launch results
- [ ] Add a 2nd traffic source OR automate a workflow (Product 19)

### Week 12 — Systemize & scale
- [ ] Document your repeatable promo process
- [ ] Decide: scale with paid traffic (Product 13)?
- [ ] Plan the next 90 days based on your data
- [ ] Celebrate progress + set new targets

---

## End-of-month review questions (ask each month)

1. What's my clearest win this month?
2. Where did I lose the most people in the funnel?
3. What's my EPC / cost per lead?
4. What ONE thing will I focus on next month?

---

*90 days of focused, consistent action will put you ahead of 95% of people who
collect tactics but never execute. Follow the plan in order. Ship daily.*
