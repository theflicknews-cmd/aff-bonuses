# Product 37 — Story-Based Email Sequence Pack

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing / Email
**Format:** 5-email "soap opera" sequence + frameworks
**Perceived value:** $67

Facts tell, stories sell. This pack gives buyers a **story-driven email sequence**
(the "soap opera sequence") that hooks new subscribers, builds a real connection,
and leads naturally to an affiliate offer — without feeling salesy.

## What's inside

- This guide — the storytelling email framework + why it works
- [`sequence.md`](sequence.md) — the 5-email fill-in-the-blank sequence

## Why story emails out-convert "tips" emails

- **Open loops** keep people reading the next email.
- **Emotion** builds connection and trust faster than information.
- **Relatability** makes the offer feel like the natural next step.
- **Memorable** — people forget tips, but they remember stories.

## The 5-email "soap opera sequence"

| Email | Theme | Job |
|-------|-------|-----|
| 1 | Set the stage | Introduce you + a high-drama hook |
| 2 | High drama / backstory | The struggle, the low point (relatable) |
| 3 | Epiphany | The turning point + the lesson/mechanism |
| 4 | Hidden benefits | What changed + introduce the solution |
| 5 | Urgency / CTA | The offer + reason to act now |

Each email ends with an **open loop** ("Tomorrow I'll show you the moment everything
changed...") so the next one gets opened.

## The storytelling formula (per email)

1. **Hook** — start mid-action or with intrigue.
2. **Story** — relatable struggle, vivid detail, emotion.
3. **Lesson** — the takeaway that ties to the reader's problem.
4. **Bridge** — connect the lesson to the offer.
5. **Open loop / CTA** — tease the next email or invite the click.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Pick a true (or true-to-life) story that mirrors your reader's journey.
2. Fill in the 5 emails (`sequence.md`).
3. Keep the open loops intact (they drive opens).
4. End the sequence on your offer + bonus stack (Product 25).
