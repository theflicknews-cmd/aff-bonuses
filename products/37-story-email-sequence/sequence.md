# The 5-Email Story Sequence

Replace `[STRUGGLE]`, `[TURNING POINT]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[RESULT]`, `[LINK]`. Tell a
true (or true-to-life) story. Keep the open loops.

---

## Email 1 — Set the Stage

**Subject:** The day I almost quit `[NICHE]`
> Hey `[FIRST NAME]`,
>
> I want to tell you something I don't share often.
>
> Not long ago, I was `[relatable starting point - frustrated, broke, stuck]`. I'd
> tried `[failed attempts]` and nothing worked.
>
> I'm telling you this because if you're feeling `[their emotion]` right now, I get
> it. And over the next few days I'm going to share exactly how things turned around
> - including the moment that changed everything.
>
> Tomorrow, I'll show you the lowest point... and what it taught me.
>
> Talk soon,
> `[YOUR NAME]`
>
> *(Open loop -> Email 2)*

---

## Email 2 — High Drama / Backstory

**Subject:** This was my breaking point
> `[FIRST NAME]`,
>
> Yesterday I mentioned almost quitting. Here's what actually happened.
>
> `[Tell the low point with vivid detail and emotion - the moment it felt hopeless.
> What you saw, felt, thought.]`
>
> I remember thinking, "Maybe this just isn't for me."
>
> But that night, something happened that flipped a switch in my head. I'll tell you
> about it tomorrow - it's the reason I'm able to write this email to you now.
>
> `[YOUR NAME]`
>
> *(Open loop -> Email 3)*

---

## Email 3 — The Epiphany

**Subject:** The moment everything changed
> `[FIRST NAME]`,
>
> So here's the turning point.
>
> `[Describe the epiphany - the realization, the thing you discovered, the
> mechanism. Make it a clear "aha".]`
>
> The lesson? `[The core insight that applies to the reader's problem.]`
>
> That one shift changed how I approached everything - and it's exactly what most
> people in `[NICHE]` get wrong.
>
> Tomorrow I'll show you what happened once I actually put it into practice (the
> results surprised even me).
>
> `[YOUR NAME]`
>
> *(Open loop -> Email 4)*

---

## Email 4 — Hidden Benefits + The Solution

**Subject:** What happened next (and the tool behind it)
> `[FIRST NAME]`,
>
> Once I `[applied the lesson]`, things changed fast: `[the results / benefits,
> including unexpected ones]`.
>
> But here's the honest part: it got so much easier when I started using `[PRODUCT]`.
> It `[core benefit]`, which meant `[outcome]` without `[pain]`.
>
> If you want the same shortcut, here's where to get it (plus the bonuses I put
> together to help you implement fast): `[LINK]`
>
> Tomorrow I'll explain why I'd act sooner rather than later.
>
> `[YOUR NAME]`
>
> *(Open loop -> Email 5)*

---

## Email 5 — Urgency + CTA

**Subject:** Why I wouldn't wait on this
> `[FIRST NAME]`,
>
> I've shared my story this week for one reason: I don't want you to spend as long
> as I did stuck in `[STRUGGLE]`.
>
> `[PRODUCT]` is the shortcut I wish I'd had. And right now you can get it with my
> bonus stack:
> - `[BONUS 1]`
> - `[BONUS 2]`
> - `[BONUS 3]`
>
> But the bonuses come down `[DEADLINE]`. After that, this exact deal is gone.
>
> Here's your link: `[LINK]`
>
> Whatever you decide, thanks for reading my story.
>
> `[YOUR NAME]`

---

*Stories build the trust that makes the offer feel like a gift, not a pitch. Keep
it honest - real beats hype every time.*
