## 4. Review Page Structure

1. **Headline:** `[PRODUCT]` Review (2026): Is It Worth It? Honest Breakdown
2. **Quick verdict box:** Rating, who it's for, price, "get it + bonuses" button.
3. **What is it:** 1-2 paragraphs.
4. **Who it's for / not for:** builds trust by disqualifying.
5. **Features + benefits:** screenshots if possible.
6. **Pros and cons:** be honest about cons.
7. **Pricing:** what you get at each tier.
8. **My bonuses:** the stack you add (-> bonus page).
9. **FAQ:** 5-7 buyer questions.
10. **Final CTA:** "Get `[PRODUCT]` + my bonuses" button + affiliate disclosure.
