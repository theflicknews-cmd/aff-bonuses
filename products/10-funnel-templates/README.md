## Why affiliates need a funnel

Sending cold traffic straight to a vendor's sales page wastes clicks and gives you
zero list. A simple funnel lets you **capture the lead, warm them up, stack your
bonus, and follow up** — turning one click into many chances to convert.

---

## The 4 funnel blueprints

### Funnel 1 — The Bridge Funnel (best for cold/paid traffic)
```
Ad/Post -> Bridge Page -> Vendor Sales Page (your link) -> Bonus delivery
```
A "bridge page" pre-frames the offer in your voice and adds your bonus before
handing off. Use when you can't capture email first.

### Funnel 2 — The Lead-Magnet Funnel (best for list building)
```
Traffic -> Opt-in Page -> Thank-You/Bridge Page -> Email Sequence -> Offer
```
Capture the email with a lead magnet (Product 6), then promote via email
(Product 4). The highest long-term ROI funnel.

### Funnel 3 — The Review Funnel (best for SEO/YouTube)
```
Search/Video -> Review Page -> Bonus Page -> Vendor Sales Page (your link)
```
Buyer-intent traffic lands on your honest review, clicks to your bonus page,
then buys through your link. Great for evergreen traffic.

### Funnel 4 — The Webinar/Launch Funnel (best for big promos)
```
Reg Page -> Confirmation -> Webinar/Replay -> Bonus Page -> Cart (your link)
```
For affiliate launches with a live or evergreen training. Highest conversion per
lead when the offer warrants it.

## Which funnel should you use?

| Your situation | Use funnel |
|----------------|-----------|
| Running paid ads | 1 — Bridge |
| Building an email list | 2 — Lead Magnet |
| Ranking content/videos | 3 — Review |
| Promoting a big launch | 4 — Webinar/Launch |
