## Why bonuses win the sale

The offer is identical no matter whose link a buyer uses. So the buyer asks: *"Who
gives me the most value for the same price?"* A relevant, high-value bonus stack
makes that answer obviously **you** — and it justifies buying *now*, through *your*
link, instead of later.

## The 5 rules of an irresistible bonus stack

**Rule 1 — Solve the NEXT problem.**
The best bonus removes the obstacle the buyer hits *right after* purchase. (Product
helps them do X? Your bonus helps them do X faster / use the result.)

**Rule 2 — Stack 3-5, not 1 or 20.**
Too few feels thin; too many feels like junk. 3-5 focused bonuses feel premium.

**Rule 3 — Anchor the value.**
Give each bonus a believable dollar value; total should dwarf the price ("$391 in
bonuses on a $27 product").

**Rule 4 — Make them complementary, not random.**
Each bonus should connect to the offer and to each other (a system, not a pile).

**Rule 5 — Add urgency + easy claiming.**
A deadline drives action; clear "how to claim" steps prevent confusion and refunds.

## The bonus page (where it converts)

Deliver the stack on a dedicated page (Product 2): headline, the stack with values,
urgency, and how-to-claim steps. That page is your conversion edge.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Identify the buyer's NEXT problem after purchasing the offer.
2. Pick 3-5 complementary bonuses from this vault.
3. Assign values; ensure the total dwarfs the price.
4. Build the bonus page (Product 2) + add a deadline.
5. Announce the stack everywhere you promote.
