# Faceless Video Production Pipeline

A repeatable, batchable workflow. Replace `[NICHE]`, `[PRODUCT]`. Use whatever tools
fit your budget - the steps don't change.

## Step 1 — Script (15 min/video)
- Use Products 1 & 7 prompts to draft.
- Structure: hook (first 3-15s) -> value -> CTA.
- Keep sentences short and spoken-friendly.
- End with a clear CTA to your link/bonus page.

## Step 2 — Voiceover
- **Option A:** AI voice tool (pick a natural voice; consistent across videos).
- **Option B:** record your own voice (still faceless).
- Tip: keep pacing energetic; pause between points.

## Step 3 — Visuals
- Screen recordings (for tutorials/demos of `[PRODUCT]`).
- Stock footage/b-roll matching the script.
- AI-generated images (Product 32) for custom visuals/thumbnails.
- Simple text-on-screen for listicles/tips.

## Step 4 — Assemble
- Drop voiceover + visuals into a video editor (or AI script-to-video tool).
- Match cuts to the narration beats.
- Add background music (low volume, royalty-free).

## Step 5 — Captions
- Auto-generate captions (most viewers watch muted).
- Style them boldly for short-form.

## Step 6 — Thumbnail + title (for YouTube)
- Bold thumbnail text (Product 32 prompts + Canva).
- Buyer-intent title (Product 21 SEO checklist).

## Step 7 — Publish + monetize
- Affiliate + bonus links in description/bio (top).
- Pinned comment with CTA.
- Disclose affiliate relationship (Product 28).

## Step 8 — Repurpose
- Cut long videos into 3-5 shorts (Product 8).
- Post across YouTube, TikTok, Reels.
- Turn the script into a blog post + email.
