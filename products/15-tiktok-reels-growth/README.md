## How short-form algorithms actually work

All three (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) reward the same thing: **watch time and
re-watches**. The algorithm tests your video on a small batch; if people watch,
re-watch, and engage, it pushes it wider. So your only jobs are:

1. **Hook in 3 seconds** (stop the scroll).
2. **Hold attention** (no dead air, fast pace, captions).
3. **Earn a loop/engagement** (a reason to rewatch, comment, or save).

Follower count barely matters — every video gets a fresh shot at virality.

## The 3-second hook rules

- Lead with the payoff or a bold claim, not an intro.
- Show motion/text on screen immediately.
- Create an open loop: "Wait for it", "Here's what nobody tells you...".
- Talk to ONE person ("If you do `[niche]`...").

## The content mix for affiliate growth

| Type | % | Purpose |
|------|---|---------|
| Value/how-to | 50% | Saves + shares = reach |
| Relatable/story | 25% | Comments + follows |
| Demo/result | 15% | Proof, sets up the offer |
| Promo/CTA | 10% | "Link in bio" to bonus page |

## Posting & growth plan

- **Post 1-3x/day** consistently (volume = more shots at virality).
- **Reuse winners:** if one hook hits, make 5 variations.
- **Batch film** (Product 8 helps you script fast).
- **Drive to "link in bio":** a link hub -> your bonus/review page (Products 2, 11).
- **Engage in the first hour:** reply to every comment to boost the video.

## Turning views into affiliate clicks

1. Build trust with value content (don't pitch every post).
2. Add a clear "link in bio" CTA on demo/promo videos.
3. Use a link hub that routes to your lead magnet (Product 6) or bonus page.
4. Capture emails so you're not renting the audience from the platform.

> Reality check: expect 20-50+ posts before you find your winning format. Consistency
> and hook quality beat everything else.
