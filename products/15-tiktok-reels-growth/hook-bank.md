# 40 Short-Form Hooks + Content Formats

Use these as your first 3 seconds. Replace `[NICHE]`, `[RESULT]`, `[PROBLEM]`.

## Curiosity hooks
1. "Here's what nobody tells you about `[NICHE]`..."
2. "I wish I knew this before I started `[NICHE]`."
3. "This `[NICHE]` trick feels illegal to know."
4. "Wait for it... this changes everything about `[TOPIC]`."
5. "The `[NICHE]` secret pros don't share for free."

## Bold-claim hooks
6. "You're doing `[TASK]` wrong. Here's the fix."
7. "Stop `[common action]` immediately."
8. "`[RESULT]` in `[timeframe]` - no `[obstacle]`."
9. "This took my `[metric]` from `[X]` to `[Y]`."
10. "Delete these `[#]` things if you want `[RESULT]`."

## Problem hooks
11. "Struggling with `[PROBLEM]`? Watch this."
12. "If `[PROBLEM]`, this is for you."
13. "Why you're stuck at `[stage]` (and how to fix it)."
14. "The real reason your `[thing]` isn't working."

## List hooks
15. "3 mistakes killing your `[RESULT]`."
16. "5 tools every `[audience]` needs in 2026."
17. "`[#]` things I'd do if I started `[NICHE]` today."
18. "My top 3 `[NICHE]` shortcuts."

## Story hooks
19. "6 months ago I was `[before]`. Today `[after]`."
20. "I almost quit `[NICHE]` until I learned this."
21. "How I went from `[before]` to `[after]`."

## Contrarian hooks
22. "Unpopular opinion: `[contrarian take]`."
23. "Everyone says `[advice]`. They're wrong."
24. "Forget `[popular thing]`. Do this instead."

## Question hooks
25. "Want `[RESULT]` without `[pain]`?"
26. "What if `[task]` only took 5 minutes?"
27. "Ever wonder how `[people]` get `[result]`?"

## Demo / result hooks
28. "Watch me `[do task]` in under a minute."
29. "This tool does `[task]` for me automatically."
30. "POV: you finally found a tool that `[benefit]`."

---

## 10 content formats that go viral in any niche

31. **The tutorial** — teach one quick win, start to finish.
32. **The mistake** — call out a common error + the fix.
33. **The before/after** — show the transformation.
34. **The list** — "3 things..." rapid-fire value.
35. **The tool demo** — show a tool getting a result (great for AI offers).
36. **The myth-bust** — challenge a belief.
37. **The story time** — personal narrative with a lesson.
38. **The "POV"** — relatable scenario your audience lives.
39. **The reaction/duet** — respond to a trend or comment.
40. **The "watch till the end"** — open loop + payoff (boosts watch time).

---

*Pick a hook, pick a format, keep it under 30 seconds, and post daily. Reuse what hits.*
