# 30-Day Content Calendar

Legend: 🎓 Value · 💬 Engagement · 🔥 Proof · 💰 Promo

## Week 1 — Build trust & introduce yourself

| Day | Type | Post idea |
|-----|------|-----------|
| 1 | 💬 | Intro post: who you help and the #1 problem you solve in `[NICHE]`. |
| 2 | 🎓 | "3 mistakes beginners make in `[NICHE]`" — one tip per slide/line. |
| 3 | 🎓 | A quick win your audience can do today (free, actionable). |
| 4 | 💬 | Ask a poll/question: "What's your biggest struggle with `[TOPIC]`?" |
| 5 | 🔥 | Behind-the-scenes of your workflow or tools you use. |
| 6 | 🎓 | Myth-bust a common belief in `[NICHE]`. |
| 7 | 💬 | Personal story: why you got into `[NICHE]` (relatable + human). |

## Week 2 — Teach & demonstrate authority

| Day | Type | Post idea |
|-----|------|-----------|
| 8 | 🎓 | Step-by-step mini tutorial on `[SUB-TOPIC]`. |
| 9 | 🔥 | Share a result/screenshot (yours or a case study) + the lesson. |
| 10 | 🎓 | "Tool I can't live without for `[TASK]`" (soft mention of offer). |
| 11 | 💬 | Hot take / mild contrarian opinion to spark comments. |
| 12 | 🎓 | A free resource list / cheat sheet (carousel works great). |
| 13 | 💰 | **Promo day:** Recommend `[PRODUCT]` — lead with the problem it solves. |
| 14 | 💬 | Recap the week + invite DMs/questions. |

## Week 3 — Deepen the relationship & pre-sell

| Day | Type | Post idea |
|-----|------|-----------|
| 15 | 🎓 | "Before/after" framework: what changes when you fix `[PROBLEM]`. |
| 16 | 🔥 | Testimonial or DM screenshot (with permission). |
| 17 | 🎓 | Answer the top question from your Day 4/11 engagement posts. |
| 18 | 💬 | "This or that" poll relevant to `[NICHE]`. |
| 19 | 🎓 | Teach a framework/acronym you can own (memorable = shareable). |
| 20 | 💰 | **Promo day:** Show `[PRODUCT]` in action — demo or walkthrough. |
| 21 | 💬 | Celebrate a follower win or shout out your community. |

## Week 4 — Convert & scale

| Day | Type | Post idea |
|-----|------|-----------|
| 22 | 🎓 | "5 things I wish I knew before starting `[NICHE]`." |
| 23 | 🔥 | Document a real-time result/experiment you're running. |
| 24 | 🎓 | Bust the #1 objection people have about `[SOLUTION/PRODUCT]`. |
| 25 | 💬 | Story post: a failure + what it taught you. |
| 26 | 💰 | **Promo day:** Bonus stack reveal — "Buy `[PRODUCT]` via my link, get X." |
| 27 | 🎓 | Round-up: "Best free + paid tools for `[NICHE]` in 2026." |
| 28 | 🔥 | Share your own progress/journey numbers transparently. |
| 29 | 💬 | Q&A / "ask me anything" about `[NICHE]`. |
| 30 | 💰 | **Promo day + urgency:** Last call on the bonus/deadline for `[PRODUCT]`. |

---

### After Day 30

Repeat the structure with fresh topics, or rotate in: live videos, collabs,
user-generated content, and seasonal angles. Keep the 40/25/20/15 mix.
