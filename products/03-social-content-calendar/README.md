# Product 3 — 30-Day Social Media Content Calendar

**Niche:** Social Media Marketing
**Format:** Day-by-day calendar + plug-and-play caption templates
**Perceived value:** $57

A done-for-you **30-day posting plan** for affiliate marketers and creators who
freeze up at "what do I post today?" Each day has a content type, a prompt, and a
fill-in-the-blank caption — built to grow an audience *and* warm them up for
affiliate offers without being spammy.

## What's inside

- [`calendar.md`](calendar.md) — the full 30-day plan (post type + idea per day).
- [`caption-templates.md`](caption-templates.md) — 15 reusable caption frameworks.

## The content mix (the 80/20 rule)

To keep an audience engaged *and* convert, the calendar follows a proven ratio:

| Type | % | Purpose |
|------|---|---------|
| 🎓 Value / educational | 40% | Build trust & save-worthy content |
| 💬 Engagement / story | 25% | Reach + relationship |
| 🔥 Proof / authority | 20% | Show results, testimonials, behind-the-scenes |
| 💰 Promo / offer | 15% | The actual affiliate pitch |

The promo days are spaced out so the feed never feels like an infomercial.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Pick your platform(s): Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Facebook.
2. Each morning, open the day's row in `calendar.md`.
3. Grab a matching caption framework from `caption-templates.md`.
4. Fill in your niche + offer. Post.
5. On 💰 promo days, link to your bonus page (Product 2) or affiliate link.

> Repurpose tip: one piece of value content can become a Reel, a carousel, a
> tweet, and an email. Don't reinvent — reformat.
