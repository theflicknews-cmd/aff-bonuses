# 15 Plug-and-Play Caption Templates

Fill in the `[brackets]`. Each works across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X
(trim for X's limit). Add 3–5 relevant hashtags where the platform rewards them.

## Value captions

**1. The Listicle**
> [#] things nobody tells you about [TOPIC]:
> 1. [point]
> 2. [point]
> 3. [point]
> Save this for later 👇 Which one surprised you?

**2. The Mistake**
> Stop doing [COMMON MISTAKE] in [NICHE].
> Here's what to do instead: [BETTER WAY].
> It took me [TIME] to learn this. Don't repeat my mistake.

**3. The Mini-Tutorial**
> How to [RESULT] in [#] steps:
> → [step 1]
> → [step 2]
> → [step 3]
> Want the full version? Comment "[KEYWORD]" and I'll send it.

**4. The Myth-Bust**
> Myth: [COMMON BELIEF].
> Truth: [REALITY].
> Here's why it matters for you: [BENEFIT].

**5. The Cheat Sheet**
> Bookmark this [TOPIC] cheat sheet 📌
> [3–5 quick tips]
> Follow for more [NICHE] shortcuts.

## Engagement captions

**6. The Poll**
> Quick question for [AUDIENCE]:
> Do you [OPTION A] or [OPTION B]?
> Drop A or B below 👇

**7. The Story**
> [TIME] ago I was [BEFORE STATE].
> Then [TURNING POINT].
> Today [AFTER STATE].
> If you're where I was, here's my advice: [TIP].

**8. The Hot Take**
> Unpopular opinion: [CONTRARIAN VIEW].
> Agree or disagree? Tell me why 👇

**9. The Question Hook**
> What's the ONE thing about [TOPIC] you wish someone explained sooner?
> I'll answer the best ones in a post this week.

**10. The Win Celebration**
> Shoutout to [PERSON/COMMUNITY] for [WIN] 🎉
> This is exactly why I do this. Who's next?

## Proof captions

**11. The Result Reveal**
> [METRIC/RESULT] in [TIMEFRAME] 📈
> Here's the one thing that moved the needle: [LESSON].
> No hype — just [WHAT YOU DID].

**12. The Testimonial**
> Got this message today and had to share 🙌
> [PASTE TESTIMONIAL]
> This is what's possible when you [ACTION].

**13. Behind-the-Scenes**
> A peek at how I actually [TASK] 👀
> The tools: [LIST]
> The process: [SHORT STEPS]

## Promo captions

**14. The Soft Pitch**
> I get asked "what do you use for [TASK]?" all the time.
> Honest answer: [PRODUCT].
> Here's why → [1–2 benefits].
> If you grab it through my link today, I'm including [BONUS]. Link in bio.

**15. The Urgency Pitch**
> ⏳ Last call: my [BONUS] for [PRODUCT] comes down [DEADLINE].
> [PRODUCT] helps you [BIG BENEFIT], and my bonus adds [EXTRA RESULT].
> Tap the link before it's gone 👉 [LINK]

---

*Mix value-heavy captions 4:1 against promo captions and your audience will
actually look forward to your offers.*
