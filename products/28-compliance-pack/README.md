## Why disclosure matters

- **Legal:** many regions (e.g., FTC in the US) require clear disclosure of paid/
  affiliate relationships.
- **Platform:** most networks and social platforms require it in their terms.
- **Trust:** honest disclosure actually *builds* trust (and conversions).

## The 3 rules of good disclosure

1. **Clear & conspicuous:** plain language, easy to notice (not buried in tiny text).
2. **Close to the link:** near the recommendation/link, not only on a separate page.
3. **Before the click:** people should know before they act on it.

## Where you need a disclosure

| Channel | Where to put it |
|---------|-----------------|
| Blog/review | Top of the post + near each link |
| YouTube | Description (high up) + verbal mention + on-screen |
| Email | Near the link / in the email |
| Social posts | In the caption (#ad / "affiliate link"), not buried in tags |
| Stories/Reels | On-screen text + verbal |
| Bonus page | Footer + near CTA |

## Common mistakes to avoid

- 🚩 Hiding the disclosure in a wall of hashtags.
- 🚩 Only disclosing on a separate "disclosure page."
- 🚩 Vague terms like "sp" or "collab" without context.
- 🚩 Income/earnings guarantees ("you WILL make $X").
- 🚩 Fake scarcity, fake testimonials, fake screenshots.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Pick the templates for your channels (`templates.md`).
2. Place them clearly, near your links, before the click.
3. Add the income/results disclaimer wherever you mention outcomes.
4. Re-check current rules for your region/platforms periodically.
