# Disclosure & Disclaimer Templates

Copy, paste, and adapt. Replace `[YOUR NAME]` / `[YOUR BRAND]`. These are starting
points, **not legal advice** - verify against current rules for your region and platforms.

## Short disclosures (near a link / in a caption)

- `#ad`
- `Affiliate link - I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.`
- `(Affiliate link)`
- `Heads up: this is an affiliate link. If you buy, I may earn a commission - thanks for the support!`

## Blog / review post (top of page)

> **Disclosure:** This post contains affiliate links. If you purchase through them,
> I may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. I only recommend products I
> believe provide value. My opinions are my own.

## Inline (next to a specific link)

> *(Affiliate link - I may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.)*

## YouTube description

> Some links below are affiliate links. If you buy through them, I may earn a
> commission at no extra cost to you. This helps support the channel - thank you!
> I only recommend products I believe in.

## Email

> Note: this email contains affiliate links. If you purchase through them I may earn
> a commission at no extra cost to you. I only share things I genuinely recommend.

## Social caption

> `[Your content]`
>
> Affiliate link in bio - I may earn a commission if you buy, at no cost to you. #ad

## Stories / Reels (on-screen text + say it)

> "Affiliate link" / "#ad" on screen, and mention it verbally: "Quick heads up,
> that's an affiliate link."

## Bonus page footer

> **Affiliate disclosure:** I am an independent affiliate, not an employee or agent
> of the product vendor. I may earn a commission if you purchase through my link, at
> no additional cost to you. The bonuses are provided by me, `[YOUR NAME]`
> (`[YOUR BRAND]`), and are not affiliated with or endorsed by the vendor.

## Income / results disclaimer (use wherever you mention results)

> **Results disclaimer:** Any results, earnings, or examples shown are not typical
> and are not a guarantee that you will achieve the same. Individual results depend
> on many factors including effort, experience, and market conditions. Nothing here
> is a promise or guarantee of income.

## Testimonial disclaimer

> Testimonials reflect individual experiences and are not a guarantee of results.
> Used with permission.

## Affiliate page boilerplate (a dedicated /disclosure page)

> **Affiliate Disclosure**
>
> `[YOUR BRAND]` participates in affiliate programs. This means we may earn
> commissions on qualifying purchases made through links on this site, at no extra
> cost to you. We only recommend products and services we believe will add value to
> our audience. Our content and opinions are our own and are not influenced by any
> advertiser or affiliate partner. If you have questions, contact us at `[email]`.

---

*Place disclosures clearly, near your links, before the click. When in doubt,
over-disclose - and consult a professional for anything significant.*
