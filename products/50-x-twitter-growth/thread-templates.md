# X Thread & Post Templates

Replace `[TOPIC]`, `[RESULT]`, `[NICHE]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[LINK]`. Keep posts punchy;
one idea per post.

## Thread frameworks

### 1. The "How-To" Thread
> **Post 1 (hook):** How to `[RESULT]` in `[timeframe]` (without `[pain]`):
> **2-6:** one step per post, concrete and actionable.
> **Final:** Recap + "If you want the shortcut, I use `[PRODUCT]` - link in my bio
> (+ a bonus). Follow `[@handle]` for more `[NICHE]` tips."

### 2. The "Mistakes" Thread
> **Hook:** `[#]` mistakes keeping you stuck in `[NICHE]` (and how to fix each):
> **Body:** mistake -> fix, one per post.
> **Final:** CTA to bio link + follow.

### 3. The "Lessons" Thread
> **Hook:** I spent `[time/money]` learning `[NICHE]`. Here are `[#]` lessons so you
> don't have to:
> **Body:** one lesson per post.
> **Final:** "Save this. Follow for more. Free `[lead magnet]` in my bio."

### 4. The "Tools/Resources" Thread
> **Hook:** `[#]` tools every `[audience]` should know in 2026:
> **Body:** tool -> what it does -> who it's for (your offer = #1).
> **Final:** "I use `[PRODUCT]` daily - link + bonus in bio."

### 5. The "Case Study" Thread
> **Hook:** How `[person]` went from `[before]` to `[after]` in `[time]`:
> **Body:** the steps + the turning point.
> **Final:** "The key tool was `[PRODUCT]` - bio link + bonus."

## Single value-post templates

- "Most people in `[NICHE]` do `[wrong thing]`. Do `[right thing]` instead. Here's why: `[1 line]`."
- "`[#]` quick `[NICHE]` tips:\n1. `[tip]`\n2. `[tip]`\n3. `[tip]`\nSave this 🔖"
- "Unpopular `[NICHE]` opinion: `[contrarian take]`. Agree?"
- "The fastest way to `[RESULT]`: `[one insight]`. That's it. That's the post."
- "If I were starting `[NICHE]` today, I'd do these 3 things: `[list]`."

## Reply strategy (borrowed reach)

- Find 5-10 bigger accounts in `[NICHE]`. Turn on notifications.
- Reply fast with genuine value (add a tip, a nuance, an example) - not "great post!".
- Aim to be one of the most useful replies = profile visits + follows.

## Pinned post options

- Your best-performing thread.
- A lead-magnet offer: "New here? Grab my free `[lead magnet]`: `[LINK]`."
- A short intro: who you help + the result + the link.
