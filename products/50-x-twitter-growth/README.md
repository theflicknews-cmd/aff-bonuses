# Product 50 — X (Twitter) Growth & Threads Playbook

**Niche:** Social Media
**Format:** Growth strategy + thread & post templates
**Perceived value:** $57

X (Twitter) is the fastest platform for building authority and relationships in the
marketing, AI, and business niches — exactly where affiliate buyers hang out. This
playbook shows buyers how to grow on X and turn followers into affiliate clicks.

## What's inside

- This guide — how X growth works + the content system
- [`thread-templates.md`](thread-templates.md) — thread frameworks + post templates

## Why X works for affiliate marketing

- **Fast feedback + reach:** good posts spread quickly via reposts.
- **Authority building:** threads showcase expertise and earn follows.
- **Relationships:** replies + DMs build real connections with peers and buyers.
- **Buyer-dense niches:** marketing, AI, business, and making-money audiences are huge here.

## How growth actually happens on X

1. **Replies first:** thoughtful replies under bigger accounts = borrowed reach + follows.
2. **Value posts:** single tips, insights, and hot takes that get saved/reposted.
3. **Threads:** deep-dive teaching that establishes authority and gets bookmarked.
4. **Consistency:** post daily; engage daily. The algorithm rewards active accounts.
5. **Profile that converts:** bio + pinned post that turn profile visitors into leads.

## The content mix

| Type | % | Purpose |
|------|---|---------|
| Single value posts | 45% | Reach + saves |
| Replies/engagement | 25% | Borrowed reach + relationships |
| Threads | 15% | Authority + bookmarks |
| Story/personal | 10% | Connection |
| Promo | 5% | CTA to offer/lead magnet |

> Promo is light here - X punishes constant link-dropping. Build trust, then point
> people to your bio link or a thread that leads to your offer.

## The profile that converts

- **Bio:** who you help + result + a CTA ("Free `[lead magnet]`: `[link]`").
- **Pinned post:** your best thread or a lead-magnet offer.
- **Link:** to your opt-in/bonus page (Products 2, 6).

## Driving clicks without getting throttled

- Lead-gen via **bio link** and **pinned post**, not raw links in every post.
- Put links in the **reply** to a thread, not always the main post.
- Use a strong thread to deliver value, then CTA at the end to your link.
- Move people to your email list (you own it).

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Optimize your bio + pinned post for lead capture.
2. Post daily using the content mix; reply under bigger accounts.
3. Publish 1-2 authority threads/week (`thread-templates.md`).
4. CTA to your bio link / lead magnet; nurture via email.
