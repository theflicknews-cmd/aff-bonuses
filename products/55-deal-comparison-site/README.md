# Product 55 — Deal & Comparison Site Model

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing / SEO
**Format:** Site model + page structure templates
**Perceived value:** $67

Some of the highest-earning affiliate sites are **deal, coupon, and comparison
hubs** — they capture people at the exact moment of buying decision. This guide
shows buyers how to build a deal/comparison site model that turns bottom-of-funnel
traffic into commissions.

## What's inside

- This guide — the model, page types, and how it captures buyers
- [`page-structures.md`](page-structures.md) — templates for deal, coupon & comparison pages

## Why this model converts so well

It targets people with the **highest buying intent** — those searching:
- "`[product]` coupon / discount / deal"
- "`[product]` pricing"
- "`[category]` comparison"
- "best `[category]` deals"

These searchers have their wallet out. You just help them decide and route them
through your link.

## The 3 page types

1. **Deal/offer pages** — current offers on tools in your niche, with your affiliate
   link + your bonus stack as the differentiator.
2. **Coupon/discount pages** — capture "`[product]` coupon" searches (only list real,
   legitimate offers/bonuses — never fake codes).
3. **Comparison/pricing pages** — side-by-side tables that help buyers choose (your
   recommended pick = your offer).

## The honest-deal principle (critical)

- Only list **real** deals, discounts, and bonuses.
- If there's no coupon, your "deal" is your **exclusive bonus stack** (Product 25) —
  that's a genuine, honest incentive fully in your control.
- Never use fake/expired codes or fake scarcity. It kills trust and can violate rules.

## How the bonus stack becomes your "deal"

You usually can't control a vendor's price. But you *can* offer something no other
affiliate does: **your bonus bundle**. So your deal pages say: "Get `[PRODUCT]`
through us and unlock `$[value]` in exclusive bonuses." That's your honest, unique deal.

## Structure that ranks + converts

- Clear, scannable tables and offer cards.
- Updated dates ("Updated `[month] 2026`") for freshness.
- Prominent CTAs to your bonus page (Product 2).
- Trust elements: honest notes, pros/cons, disclosure (Product 28).
- Internal links between deal, comparison, and review pages (Product 11).

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Pick a niche with tools that have ongoing offers/recurring commissions.
2. Build deal, coupon, and comparison pages (`page-structures.md`).
3. Make your **bonus stack** the differentiator on every page.
4. Target buyer-intent keywords (Product 31) + keep offers honest and current.
