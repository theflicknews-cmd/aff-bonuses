# Deal, Coupon & Comparison Page Templates

Replace `[PRODUCT]`, `[CATEGORY]`, `[VALUE]`, `[LINK]`. List only REAL deals/bonuses.

---

## Template A — Deal/Offer Page

- **H1:** `[Product]` Deal 2026: Best Offer + Exclusive Bonuses
- **Updated:** `[Month] 2026`
- **Offer card (top):**
  > **`[Product]`** — `[current price/offer]`
  > 🎁 Plus our exclusive bonus stack ($`[VALUE]` value) when you buy through us.
  > **[Get the Deal + Bonuses]** -> `[LINK]`
- **What you get:** quick feature/benefit list.
- **Why buy through us:** your bonus stack (the real differentiator).
- **Honest note:** is there an actual discount, or is the bonus the deal? Say so.
- **FAQ + affiliate disclosure.**

## Template B — Coupon/Discount Page

- **H1:** `[Product]` Coupon & Discount Codes (`[Month] 2026`)
- **Honest status line:** "Active offers below. We only list verified deals - no fake
  codes."
- **Offers list:**
  | Offer | Details | How to get it |
  |-------|---------|---------------|
  | `[real discount/trial]` | `[details]` | `[Claim ->]` `[LINK]` |
  | Exclusive bonus stack | $`[VALUE]` in bonuses | `[Get it ->]` `[LINK]` |
- **If no code exists:** "There's no public coupon right now - but you can still get
  extra value through our exclusive bonus bundle below."
- **Disclosure.**

- **Our pick:** your recommended offer + your bonus stack.
- **FAQ + disclosure.**

## Trust & freshness checklist (every page)

- [ ] "Updated `[month] 2026`" date shown
- [ ] Only real, current offers/bonuses listed
- [ ] No fake/expired codes or fake countdowns
- [ ] Clear CTA to your bonus page (Product 2)
- [ ] Honest pros/cons (don't oversell)
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure (Product 28)
- [ ] Internal links to your reviews/comparisons (Product 11)

## SEO targets (buyer-intent keywords)

- `[product]` coupon / discount / promo code / deal
- `[product]` pricing / cost / plans
- `[category]` comparison / `[A]` vs `[B]`
- best `[category]` deals 2026

buyers routed through your link. Keep it real and keep it current.*
