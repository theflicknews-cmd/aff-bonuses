# Product 20 — Affiliate Offer Scorecard & Niche Selection Kit

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing
**Format:** Scoring system + niche selection framework
**Perceived value:** $57

Promoting the wrong offer wastes your best traffic. This kit gives buyers a
**repeatable scoring system** to vet any offer (and a framework to pick a niche)
so they only promote winners.

## What's inside

- This guide — the niche selection framework + how to score offers
- [`scorecard.md`](scorecard.md) — the printable offer scorecard + worksheet

## Part 1 — Niche selection (the 3-circle framework)

Pick a niche where these overlap:

```
   INTEREST            DEMAND
 (you'll stick      (people actively
   with it)            buying)
        \             /
         \           /
        COMMISSIONS
     (offers worth promoting)
```

- **Interest:** you can create content on it for months without burning out.
- **Demand:** people are searching and spending (check search volume, active groups,
  number of offers in the space).
- **Commissions:** decent payouts, ideally with recurring or upsells.

Avoid niches with passion but no money, or money but no offers you can stand behind.

## Part 2 — The offer scorecard (score 1-5 each)

Score every offer on these 8 factors before promoting:

| # | Factor | What to check |
|---|--------|---------------|
| 1 | Relevance | Does it match your audience's #1 problem? |
| 2 | Commission | % and $ per sale; recurring? |
| 3 | Funnel value | Upsells/downsells that pay you too |
| 4 | Conversion proof | Sales page quality, EPC, vendor reputation |
| 5 | Refund rate | Low = a quality product |
| 6 | Demand | Are people actively buying this type? |
| 7 | Competition fit | Can you stand out (bonuses/angle)? |
| 8 | Trust | Would you genuinely recommend it? |

**Scoring:**
- 32-40: Strong — promote it.
- 24-31: Decent — promote with a strong bonus stack/angle.
- Below 24: Skip it. Your traffic is too valuable.

## Part 3 — Red flags (auto-skip)

- 🚩 High refund rate or lots of complaints
- 🚩 Hype-y, false-promise sales page you can't stand behind
- 🚩 No tracking/poor reporting from the vendor
- 🚩 Tiny commission with no upsells
- 🚩 Saturated with no way for you to differentiate

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Use the 3-circle framework to confirm your niche.
2. Score 3-5 candidate offers with the scorecard (`scorecard.md`).
3. Promote the highest scorer; add bonuses to win the click.
4. Re-score periodically — offers and refund rates change.
