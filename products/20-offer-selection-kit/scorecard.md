## Verdict

- [ ] 32-40 — Strong. Promote it.
- [ ] 24-31 — Decent. Promote with a strong bonus stack + unique angle.
- [ ] Below 24 — Skip. Find a better offer.

## Red-flag check (any checked = reconsider)

- [ ] High refund rate / complaints
- [ ] Hype-y or misleading sales page
- [ ] Poor vendor tracking/reporting
- [ ] Tiny commission, no upsells
- [ ] Saturated with no differentiation
