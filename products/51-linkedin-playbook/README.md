# Product 51 — LinkedIn Affiliate Marketing Playbook

**Niche:** Social Media (B2B / professional)
**Format:** Strategy + post templates
**Perceived value:** $57

LinkedIn is the highest-trust, highest-income-audience platform — perfect for
promoting B2B tools, software, courses, and high-ticket offers. Competition for
attention is lower than on consumer platforms. This playbook shows buyers how to
build authority on LinkedIn and convert it into affiliate sales.

## What's inside

- This guide — how LinkedIn growth works + the professional positioning angle
- [`post-templates.md`](post-templates.md) — profile + post + outreach templates

## Why LinkedIn is a hidden affiliate goldmine

- **Buyer-rich audience:** professionals and businesses with budgets.
- **High trust:** real names, real careers — recommendations carry weight.
- **Lower competition:** fewer creators "selling" than on IG/TikTok.
- **Perfect for B2B/SaaS/high-ticket:** marketing tools, AI software, courses,
  coaching (pairs with Products 23, 41).

## Optimize your profile (it's a landing page)

- **Headline:** who you help + the result ("I help `[audience]` `[result]`").
- **Banner:** reinforce your positioning + a CTA.
- **About:** your story + who you serve + a soft CTA to your link/lead magnet.
- **Featured section:** pin your lead magnet, best post, or bonus page.

## The content system

| Type | % | Example |
|------|---|---------|
| Insight/teaching | 45% | A lesson or framework for `[audience]` |
| Story/experience | 25% | A professional lesson learned |
| Opinion/POV | 15% | A take on a trend/tool in your niche |
| Engagement | 10% | Questions, polls |
| Promo | 5% | Soft recommendation -> your offer |

LinkedIn rewards **native text posts** and **documents (carousels)**. Hook in the
first 1-2 lines (before "see more"), short paragraphs, end with a question.

## Converting connections into clients

1. **Content builds authority** — consistent value posts.
2. **Engagement builds relationships** — comment on your audience's posts.
3. **DMs (value-first)** — connect, build rapport, then help (Product 27).
4. **Lead magnet** — drive to a free resource (Products 6, 36).
5. **Soft promo** — recommend tools you use; link via featured/DM, not spammy posts.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Optimize your profile as a conversion page.
2. Post 3-5x/week using the content mix.
3. Engage genuinely with your target audience daily.
4. Drive to a lead magnet; recommend offers (especially B2B/SaaS/high-ticket).
