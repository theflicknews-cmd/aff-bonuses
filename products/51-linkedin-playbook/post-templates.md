# LinkedIn Templates

Replace `[AUDIENCE]`, `[RESULT]`, `[NICHE]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[LINK]`. Hook in line 1.

## Profile templates

**Headline:**
- `Helping [audience] [result] | [credential/proof] | Free [lead magnet] below`
- `[Niche] for [audience] | I share what actually works | DM "[keyword]"`

**About (opening):**
> I help `[audience]` `[result]` without `[pain]`.
> `[1-2 lines of story/credibility]`.
> I post about `[topics]` here `[#]`x a week. Grab my free `[lead magnet]`: `[LINK]`.

**Featured:** pin your lead magnet, best post, and bonus/review page.

## Post templates (native text)

**1. Insight/teaching**
> `[Counterintuitive one-line hook]`
>
> Most `[audience]` think `[belief]`.
>
> But here's what actually works: `[insight]`.
>
> `[2-3 short supporting points]`
>
> What's worked for you? 👇

**2. Story/lesson**
> `[Hook: a moment or result]`
>
> `[Short story of a struggle + turning point]`
>
> The lesson: `[takeaway that helps the reader]`.
>
> `[Question to drive comments]`

**3. Framework/list**
> `[#]` ways to `[RESULT]`:
>
> 1. `[point]`
> 2. `[point]`
> 3. `[point]`
>
> Save this. Which would you add?

**4. Opinion/POV**
> Hot take: `[contrarian view about NICHE/a tool]`.
>
> Here's why: `[reasoning]`.
>
> Agree or disagree?

**5. Soft promo**
> I get asked what I use for `[task]` a lot.
>
> Honest answer: `[PRODUCT]`. It `[benefit]`, which saves me `[time/effort]`.
>
> (Not for everyone - best if you `[fit]`.) Link + a bonus in my featured section.

## Document/carousel idea

> Title slide: "`[#]` `[NICHE]` mistakes" -> one mistake+fix per slide -> final slide
> CTA to your lead magnet. Documents get strong reach on LinkedIn.

## Connection + DM templates (value-first)

**Connection request:**
> Hi `[NAME]` - love your content on `[topic]`. Always happy to connect with fellow
> `[audience]`.

**After connecting (no pitch):**
> Thanks for connecting, `[NAME]`! What are you focused on with `[NICHE]` right now?
> Happy to share anything useful.

**Soft offer (after rapport):**
> Since you mentioned `[goal]` - the tool that helped me most was `[PRODUCT]`. Happy
> to send the link (+ a bonus) if useful, no pressure.

## Engagement routine

- Comment thoughtfully on 5-10 posts/day from your target audience + bigger voices.
- Reply to every comment on your posts (boosts reach).
- Connect with people who engage with your content.

---

*LinkedIn rewards consistency, native text/documents, and genuine engagement. High-
trust audience = great for B2B, SaaS, and high-ticket affiliate offers.*
