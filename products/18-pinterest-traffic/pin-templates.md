## 30-Day Pinterest Plan

### Week 1 — Setup
- [ ] Create/convert to a business account
- [ ] Optimize profile name + bio with keywords
- [ ] Create 5 keyword-named boards
- [ ] Set up the page you'll pin to (review/blog/bonus)
- [ ] Design your first 5 pins

### Week 2 — Build the habit
- [ ] Pin 5-10/day (mix your pins + relevant repins)
- [ ] Create 2-3 pin designs per piece of content
- [ ] Note which pins get the most saves/clicks

### Week 3 — Optimize
- [ ] Make more pins like your top performers
- [ ] Add fresh keywords to descriptions
- [ ] Pin to the most relevant board each time

### Week 4 — Scale
- [ ] Increase to 10-15 pins/day if time allows
- [ ] Refresh older content with new pin designs
- [ ] Review analytics: top pins, top boards, top clicks
- [ ] Plan next month around what's working

---

*Pinterest rewards consistency and keywords. Pin to your own content and let it
compound for months.*
