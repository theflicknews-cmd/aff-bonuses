# Product 18 — Pinterest Traffic Playbook

**Niche:** Social Media / Traffic
**Format:** Strategy + pin templates + 30-day plan
**Perceived value:** $47

Pinterest is a search engine disguised as a social network — pins drive clicks for
months or years, making it perfect for evergreen affiliate traffic. This playbook
shows buyers how to turn Pinterest into a hands-off click machine.

## What's inside

- This guide — how Pinterest works + the affiliate traffic system
- [`pin-templates.md`](pin-templates.md) — pin title/description swipes + a 30-day plan

## Why Pinterest works for affiliates

- **Long shelf life:** a pin can drive traffic for years (vs hours on other apps).
- **Buyer intent:** people search Pinterest to *plan and buy*.
- **Low competition for effort:** consistency beats follower count.
- **Visual:** great for "best `[product]`", how-tos, and listicles.

## The golden rule (compliance)

Most affiliate programs and Pinterest prefer you **pin to your own content**
(blog post, review, bonus page) rather than dropping raw affiliate links. Pin ->
your page -> affiliate link. Safer, and it builds your asset.

## The Pinterest traffic system

1. **Set up a business account** (free analytics + rich pins).
2. **Optimize your profile** with keywords your audience searches.
3. **Create keyword-rich boards** around your niche sub-topics.
4. **Design vertical pins** (2:3 ratio, bold text overlay, clear benefit).
5. **Pin consistently** (5-15/day, mix fresh + repins).
6. **Link each pin to your content** (review/blog/bonus page).
7. **Double down on top performers** (make more pins like your winners).

## Pin design that gets clicks

- Vertical (1000x1500px), bright, high-contrast.
- Bold text overlay stating the benefit/result.
- Readable on mobile (most traffic is mobile).
- 2-3 pin designs per piece of content (test which wins).

## What to pin (content types)

| Content | Example pin |
|---------|-------------|
| "Best of" list | "7 Best `[tools]` for `[goal]`" |
| How-to | "How to `[result]` in `[timeframe]`" |
| Review | "Honest `[product]` Review (2026)" |
| Checklist/freebie | "Free `[lead magnet]`: `[result]`" |
| Comparison | "`[A]` vs `[B]`: Which Wins?" |

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Set up and optimize your account + boards.
2. Create a blog/review/bonus page to pin to (Products 2, 11).
3. Design 2-3 pins per piece using the swipe titles.
4. Follow the 30-day plan in `pin-templates.md`.
