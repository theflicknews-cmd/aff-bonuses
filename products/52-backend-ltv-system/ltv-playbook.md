# LTV Playbook: Math + Follow-Up Sequences

## The LTV math (why it matters)

- **Buyer LTV** = avg total commissions you earn per buyer over their lifetime.
- If LTV = `$[X]`, you can afford to spend up to ~`$[X]` to acquire a buyer (minus
  your margin) - far more than affiliates who only count the first sale.

| | Front-end only | With backend |
|--|----------------|--------------|
| First sale commission | $30 | $30 |
| Offer #2 (30% buy) | - | +$30 |
| Recurring (40% stay 6mo) | - | +$36 |
| High-ticket (5% buy) | - | +$50 |
| **Approx LTV** | **$30** | **~$146** |

Same buyer, ~5x the value - just from following up.

## Buyer onboarding sequence (first 7 days)

**Email 1 (immediately):** Deliver bonuses + thank them + the #1 first step.
> "Thanks for grabbing `[PRODUCT]`! Here are your bonuses: `[links]`. Do this first:
> `[quick win]`."

**Email 2 (day 2):** Help them get a quick win (reduces refunds, builds trust).
**Email 3 (day 4):** Share a tip to get more from their purchase.
**Email 4 (day 6):** Introduce the natural next tool (offer #2).

## Backend promo sequence (offer #2+)

**Email 1:** "Now that you've got `[PRODUCT]`, the next thing that'll help is
`[OFFER #2]` because `[reason]`." + link + bonus.
**Email 2:** Proof/use-case for offer #2.
**Email 3:** Soft urgency + bonus deadline.

## Ongoing buyer nurture (keep LTV growing)

- Email buyers valuable content regularly (not just offers).
- Share new tools/updates relevant to their journey.
- Re-promote recurring offers' value (reduce churn - Product 41).
- Periodically present a premium/high-ticket option (Product 23).

## Buyer-list rules

- [ ] Tag buyers separately from free subscribers
- [ ] Always deliver/onboard fast (refunds kill LTV)
- [ ] Promote only genuinely useful "next" offers
- [ ] Track LTV per buyer over time
- [ ] Treat buyers as VIPs (they fund the business)
