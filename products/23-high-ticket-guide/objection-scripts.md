# 10 High-Ticket Objection Scripts

Adapt these for emails, DMs, content, or live conversations. Replace `[PRODUCT]`,
`[RESULT]`, `[PRICE]`. The goal is to reframe honestly, never to pressure.

---

**1. "It's too expensive."**
> I understand. The real question isn't the price - it's the cost of staying where
> you are. If `[PRODUCT]` helps you `[RESULT]`, it pays for itself. And there's
> `[guarantee/payment plan]` so it's lower risk than it looks.

**2. "I can't afford it right now."**
> Totally fair. Two things: there's often a `[payment plan]`, and the bigger cost is
> usually another year of `[problem]`. If now genuinely isn't the time, I'd rather
> you wait than stretch - but if it's fear, not finances, let's talk it through.

**3. "I need to think about it."**
> Of course - this is a real decision. Can I ask what specifically you want to think
> through? Often it's one question I can answer right now: `[address it]`.

**4. "Will this work for me?"**
> Great question. It works best for `[fit criteria]` - does that sound like you? If
> so, here's why: `[reason + proof]`. If not, I'll tell you honestly.

**5. "I've been burned before."**
> I get it, there's a lot of hype out there. That's why `[PRODUCT]` has `[proof /
> guarantee / track record]`. I only recommend things I'd stand behind - here's my
> honest take: `[balanced view]`.

**6. "I don't have time."**
> The system is built to save time, not add to it - `[how]`. Most people start with
> just `[small commitment]`. Doing nothing is what actually costs you time.

**7. "I'm not techy enough."**
> You don't need to be. It includes `[support/onboarding]`, and I'll add `[bonus:
> setup help]` if you go through me. We'll get you set up step by step.

**8. "Can't I just figure it out myself / use free stuff?"**
> You can - free info is everywhere. What you're paying for is the shortcut, the
> structure, and the support, so you skip months of trial and error. Time is the one
> thing you can't get back.

**9. "What if I don't get results?"**
> Results depend on doing the work, but you're protected by `[guarantee]`, and I'm
> adding `[bonus support]` to help you actually implement. You're not on your own.

**10. "Let me check with my partner/team."**
> Smart - it's a real investment. Here's a quick summary you can share: `[what it
> is, what it costs, what it returns]`. Happy to jump on a call with both of you.

---

## The reframe that underpins all of these

Shift the conversation from **price** to **cost of inaction** and **return**. People
don't buy because something is cheap - they buy because the value clearly exceeds
the price and they trust you. Lead with trust and honesty; the close takes care of itself.
