# Product 23 — High-Ticket Affiliate Promotion Guide

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing
**Format:** Strategy guide + objection scripts
**Perceived value:** $77

Same effort, bigger payday. Promoting high-ticket offers ($500-$5,000+) can earn
more from one sale than dozens of small ones. This guide shows buyers how to
promote high-ticket affiliate offers the right way.

## What's inside

- This guide — the high-ticket model + the trust-first sales process
- [`objection-scripts.md`](objection-scripts.md) — responses to the 10 big objections

## Low-ticket vs high-ticket (the math)

| | Low-ticket | High-ticket |
|--|-----------|-------------|
| Price | $7-$97 | $500-$5,000+ |
| Commission/sale | $3-$50 | $250-$2,500+ |
| Sales needed for $5k | 100-1,000+ | 2-20 |
| Trust required | Low | High |
| Sales process | Fast | Longer, relationship-based |

Fewer sales, more trust, bigger checks. The whole game is **building trust** before
the ask.

## The trust-first process

1. **Lead with value** — content, a webinar, or a free resource that genuinely helps.
2. **Build the relationship** — email nurture, replies, live calls/Q&A.
3. **Demonstrate the transformation** — case studies, proof, your own results.
4. **Invite, don't pressure** — high-ticket buyers decide deliberately.
5. **Support the close** — many high-ticket offers have a call/application step.

## Where high-ticket affiliate offers live

- Coaching/mentorship programs
- Done-for-you services and software suites
- Masterminds and certifications
- Premium course bundles
Many run affiliate programs with webinars or application funnels you plug into.

## Funnels that fit high-ticket

- **Webinar funnel** (Product 12) — teach, build trust, present the offer.
- **Application/call funnel** — content -> application -> sales call (vendor often handles the call).
- **Email nurture** (Product 4) — long-form relationship building before the offer.

## Your bonus stack still matters

High-ticket buyers love value. Stack premium bonuses (1:1 onboarding call, your
templates, a private group) to tip the decision your way.

## Mindset shifts for high-ticket

- Focus on **fit**, not pressure — qualify, don't convince.
- Expect a **longer cycle** — nurture over days/weeks.
- **Quality traffic > volume** — you need fewer, better-qualified leads.
- **Answer objections proactively** (see `objection-scripts.md`).

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Find a high-ticket offer with a proven funnel + good support.
2. Build a webinar or nurture sequence to warm leads.
3. Lead with massive value; invite the right people.
4. Use the objection scripts to remove friction.
