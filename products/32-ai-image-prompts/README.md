## Anatomy of a strong image prompt

`[Subject]` + `[style/medium]` + `[composition/framing]` + `[lighting/mood]` +
`[color]` + `[details]` + `[aspect ratio/quality]`

> Example: "A confident creator at a laptop, flat vector illustration, centered
> composition, bright studio lighting, bold orange and navy palette, clean
> background with space for text, --ar 16:9"

## Tips that instantly improve results

- **Be specific** — vague prompts give generic images.
- **Name a style** — "flat vector", "3D render", "photorealistic", "minimal line art".
- **Leave space for text** — for thumbnails/graphics, ask for "negative space" or
  "clean background on the left for text overlay".
- **Set aspect ratio** — 16:9 (YouTube/landscape), 9:16 (Reels/Stories), 1:1
  (feed), 2:3 (Pinterest).
- **Iterate** — generate variations, then refine the best.
- **Add text separately** — most image AIs are weak at text; add headlines in Canva.

## Compliance & quality notes

- Check each tool's commercial-use license before using images in promotions.
- Avoid generating real people's likenesses or trademarked logos.
- Always add your own text/branding for a polished, on-brand result.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Pick a prompt matching your asset (thumbnail, ad, graphic).
2. Fill the `[brackets]` + set the aspect ratio.
3. Generate, pick the best, refine.
4. Add headline text + your branding in Canva/editor.
