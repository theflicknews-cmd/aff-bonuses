# Product 46 — 5-Day Challenge Launch Kit

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing / Launches
**Format:** Challenge funnel blueprint + daily scripts
**Perceived value:** $77

Challenges are one of the highest-engagement promo mechanisms — people *participate*
instead of just watching, which builds massive trust and primes them to buy. This
kit gives buyers a complete **5-day challenge funnel** to promote any affiliate offer.

## What's inside

- This guide — how challenge funnels work + the structure
- [`challenge-scripts.md`](challenge-scripts.md) — daily prompts, emails, and the pitch

## Why challenges convert so well

- **Participation = commitment** — people who *do* the work trust the solution.
- **Quick wins = belief** — each day they get a small result, building momentum.
- **Built-in deadline** — the challenge dates create natural urgency.
- **Community energy** — group momentum pulls people toward the offer.
- **Segmentation** — engaged participants are your hottest buyers.

## The challenge funnel flow

```
Promo -> Registration page -> Daily lessons (5 days) -> Daily engagement
   -> Day 5 pitch (your offer + bonus) -> Follow-up for non-buyers
```

## Designing your 5-day challenge

- **Pick ONE outcome** participants can achieve in 5 days (e.g., "Set up your first
  affiliate promo in 5 days").
- **One small win per day** that builds toward the outcome.
- **Day 5 reveals** that the offer is the shortcut to do it all faster/better.
- **Host it** via email + a free group (FB group/Discord) or just email.

## The 5-day arc

| Day | Theme | Win | Sets up |
|-----|-------|-----|---------|
| 1 | Foundation | Quick setup win | The big picture |
| 2 | Core skill | First real result | "There's a faster way" |
| 3 | Momentum | Visible progress | The gap the offer fills |
| 4 | Breakthrough | The "aha" result | The offer (soft intro) |
| 5 | Putting it together | Complete the outcome | The pitch + bonuses |

## Where the offer fits

Throughout, you teach the manual way; on Day 4-5 you reveal that `[PRODUCT]` does it
faster/easier. The pitch feels earned because they've already gotten results.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Define a 5-day outcome that naturally leads to your offer.
2. Use the daily scripts (`challenge-scripts.md`) for lessons + emails.
3. Drive registrations (Product 10 reg page) + host in email/a free group.
4. Pitch on Day 5 with your bonus stack (Product 25); follow up after.
