# 5-Day Challenge Scripts

Replace `[OUTCOME]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[NICHE]`, `[BONUS]`, `[LINK]`. Each day = a short
lesson + a daily email to drive people back.

## Registration page copy

> **Free 5-Day `[OUTCOME]` Challenge**
> In just 5 days, you'll `[outcome]` - step by step, with me guiding you each day.
> Totally free. Starts `[DATE]`.
> ✅ `[Day 1 benefit]` ✅ `[Day 3 benefit]` ✅ `[Day 5 benefit]`
> **[Save My Spot]** (enter email)

## Pre-challenge email (after signup)

**Subject:** You're in! The `[OUTCOME]` Challenge starts `[DATE]`
> Welcome! Over 5 days you'll `[outcome]`. Watch your inbox each morning. Day 1 drops
> `[DATE]`. To get the most out of it: `[one prep step / join the group: link]`.

---

## Day 1 — Foundation

**Lesson:** The big picture of `[OUTCOME]` + today's quick setup win: `[task]`.
**Email subject:** Day 1 is live - let's set you up
> Today's mission: `[small task]`. It takes ~15 min and sets the foundation. Reply
> "DONE" when you finish 👇 `[link to lesson]`

## Day 2 — Core Skill

**Lesson:** Teach the core skill; participants get their first real result.
**Email subject:** Day 2: your first real win
> Yesterday you set up; today you `[result]`. Here's how -> `[link]`. (Tomorrow gets
> exciting - we start building momentum.)

## Day 3 — Momentum

**Lesson:** Build on days 1-2; visible progress. Plant: "doing this manually is slow."
**Email subject:** Day 3: momentum (you're ahead of most people)
> Look how far you've come in 3 days. Today: `[task]`. Notice how `[the tedious part]`
> takes time? Keep that in mind - I'll show you a shortcut soon. `[link]`

## Day 4 — Breakthrough

**Lesson:** The "aha" result + soft intro of `[PRODUCT]` as the accelerator.
**Email subject:** Day 4: the breakthrough
> Today you'll `[aha result]`. Honestly, this is where most people realize they want
> to go faster - which is exactly what `[PRODUCT]` does (more on that tomorrow).
> Today's steps -> `[link]`

## Day 5 — Putting It Together + Pitch

**Lesson:** Complete the `[OUTCOME]`; reveal the offer as the shortcut to scale it.
**Email subject:** Day 5: you did it 🎉 (+ your next step)
> You just `[outcome]` in 5 days - amazing work! If you want to `[scale/automate/go
> faster]`, `[PRODUCT]` is the tool I'd use. And if you grab it through my link
> today, I'm including: `[BONUS LIST]`.
> But the bonuses close `[DEADLINE]`. Here's your link -> `[LINK]`

---

## Post-challenge follow-up (days 6-8, for non-buyers)

- **Day 6:** Recap their win + testimonial from another participant + link.
- **Day 7:** Address the top objection + remind of the bonus deadline.
- **Day 8:** Final call - bonuses end tonight (Product 4 last-call swipe).

## Daily group prompts (if hosting in a free group)

- "Drop a ✅ when you finish today's task!"
- "Share your result - I'll give feedback 👇"
- "Biggest takeaway from today?"
- (Day 5) "Who's ready to take this further? Here's how 👉 `[LINK]`"

---

*People trust what they participate in. Give them 5 quick wins, then offer the
shortcut to more. Challenges convert because the buyer already proved it works.*
