## What's inside

## The site architecture that ranks

```
HOMEPAGE (positions you + links to best content)
   |
   +-- "Best [category]" pillar pages   <- highest buyer intent
   +-- Individual product reviews       <- "[product] review"
   +-- Comparison posts                 <- "[A] vs [B]"
   +-- Helpful how-to / info posts       <- builds topical authority
   +-- About / Disclosure / Contact     <- trust + compliance
```

Internal links flow from info posts -> reviews -> "best of" pillars, concentrating
authority on your money pages.

## The 4 money-page types (ranked by buyer intent)

1. **"Best [category] for [audience]" pillar** — *highest intent.* Compares your top
   picks; #1 pick is your main offer.
2. **Single product review** — "`[product]` review" searchers are close to buying.
3. **Comparison ("X vs Y")** — captures people deciding between two options.
4. **Alternatives ("best `[product]` alternatives")** — captures dissatisfied users.

## Monetization stack

- Affiliate links on every review + bonus page (Product 2)
- Email capture with a lead magnet (Product 6) on every post
- Comparison tables with affiliate CTAs
- Optional display ads once traffic grows

## Launch checklist (for the affiliate)

- [ ] Add About, Affiliate Disclosure, Privacy, Contact pages

- [ ] Add internal links + email opt-ins
