# Review Site Page Templates

Fill the `[brackets]`. Each is an SEO-friendly outline you can write with Product 1's prompts.

---

## Template A — "Best [Category]" Pillar Page

1. **Title (H1):** Best `[category]` for `[audience]` in 2026 (Ranked & Reviewed)
2. **Intro:** the problem + how you tested/chose (builds trust).
3. **Quick comparison table:** name, best for, price, rating, CTA button.
4. **#1 Pick — `[Product]`:** why it wins, key features, pros/cons, CTA + bonus link.
5. **#2-#5 picks:** shorter blurbs each with CTA.
6. **How to choose:** buyer's guide section (great for SEO).
7. **FAQ:** 5-7 questions.
8. **Verdict:** restate the #1 pick + CTA.
9. **Affiliate disclosure.**

---

## Template B — Single Product Review

1. **Title:** `[Product]` Review (2026): Honest Pros, Cons & Verdict
2. **Verdict box (top):** rating, who it's for, price, "Get it + bonuses" button.
3. **What is `[product]`?**
4. **Who is it for / not for?**
5. **Key features (with benefits + screenshots).**
6. **Pricing & plans.**
7. **Pros and cons (be honest).**
8. **My exclusive bonuses (-> bonus page).**
9. **Alternatives (link to comparison posts).**
10. **FAQ.**
11. **Final verdict + CTA + disclosure.**

---

## Template C — Comparison ("X vs Y")

1. **Title:** `[A]` vs `[B]`: Which Is Better for `[use case]`? (2026)
2. **Quick answer:** one-line recommendation up top.
3. **Comparison table:** features side by side.
4. **`[A]` overview** — strengths/weaknesses.
5. **`[B]` overview** — strengths/weaknesses.
6. **Head-to-head:** price, ease, features, support.
7. **Which should you pick?** (by scenario).
8. **CTA to your recommended pick + bonus + disclosure.**

---

## Template D — "Alternatives" Post

1. **Title:** `[#]` Best `[Product]` Alternatives in 2026
2. **Intro:** why someone looks for alternatives (price, features, etc.).
3. **List of alternatives:** each with best-for, pros/cons, CTA.
4. **Comparison table.**
5. **Our top recommendation + CTA + disclosure.**

---

## Required trust pages (write once)

- **Affiliate Disclosure:** "This site contains affiliate links. We may earn a
  commission at no extra cost to you. Our opinions are our own."
- **About:** who you are, why you review `[niche]`, your testing process.
- **Privacy Policy & Contact.**
