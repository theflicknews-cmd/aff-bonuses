## What's inside

- This guide — how to use seasonal angles + the promo calendar
- [`seasonal-swipes.md`](seasonal-swipes.md) — the calendar + email/social swipes

## Why seasonal promos work

- **Built-in urgency** — the date itself is the deadline.
- **Buyer mindset** — people already expect deals around key dates.
- **Fresh angles** — same offer, new reason to buy.
- **Plannable** — you can prepare campaigns weeks ahead (Product 29).

## The 4 types of seasonal angles

1. **Holidays/sales events** — Black Friday, New Year, etc. (discount + bonus urgency).
2. **"New start" moments** — New Year, new quarter, "new you" (goal-driven).
3. **Cultural/seasonal themes** — summer, back-to-school, spring cleaning (themed angles).
4. **Manufactured events** — your own "anniversary sale", "flash bonus weekend".

> You don't need a real discount to use a seasonal angle - an **expiring bonus stack**
> (Product 25) creates the same urgency, fully in your control.

## How to plan a year of promos

- Map the big dates to your niche (see calendar in `seasonal-swipes.md`).
- Pick 1 main promo per month minimum.
- Prepare swipes 2-4 weeks ahead (Product 29 timeline).
- Reuse winners year over year (just refresh the angle).

## Compliance note

- Only claim discounts/urgency that are real.
- If the "deal" is your bonus stack expiring, say exactly that.
- No fake countdowns or false scarcity.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Open the calendar; pick the next 1-2 dates to plan around.
2. Grab the matching email + social swipes.
3. Fill in your offer + (real) urgency/bonus deadline.
4. Schedule with the launch timeline (Product 29).
