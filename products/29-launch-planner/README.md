# Product 29 — Affiliate Launch & Promo Planner

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing
**Format:** Launch planning system + timeline template
**Perceived value:** $57

The biggest affiliate paydays come from **launches** — coordinated promotions
around a product's open/close dates (the bread and butter of WarriorPlus, JVZoo,
and LaunchpadJV). This planner gives buyers the system to plan and execute a
profitable launch promotion.

## What's inside

- This guide — the launch promo timeline + leaderboard tactics
- [`launch-timeline.md`](launch-timeline.md) — a day-by-day fill-in template

## Why launches are so profitable

- **Built-in urgency** (cart open/close, early-bird pricing, expiring bonuses).
- **High buyer energy** (everyone's promoting, the market is primed).
- **Leaderboard prizes** (vendors reward top affiliates with cash/bonuses).
- **Stacked commissions** (front-end + multiple upsells in the funnel).

## The launch promo timeline (overview)

| Phase | When | Your job |
|-------|------|----------|
| Pre-launch | 7-14 days before | Warm your audience, build anticipation |
| Open cart | Launch day | Hit hard: email, social, video |
| Mid-launch | Days 2-4 | Value, proof, objection-handling |
| Cart close | Final 24-48h | Urgency, last-call, bonus deadline |
| Post-launch | After close | Downsell, thank, debrief |

## Pre-launch (the secret to a big launch day)

Most of the win happens *before* the cart opens:
1. Get on the vendor's JV/affiliate page; grab assets + dates.
2. Tease the topic/problem to your list and social (no pitch yet).
3. Deliver value content that pre-frames the offer.
4. Build your bonus stack + bonus page (Products 2, 25).
5. Schedule your launch emails (Product 4) and content (Products 3, 7).

## Leaderboard tactics (if you're competing for prizes)

- Concentrate your biggest push on day 1 (when most prizes track).
- Add an extra "fast-action" bonus for early buyers.
- Email more than once on open and close days (segment by openers/clickers).
- Use every channel simultaneously on the key days.

## Post-launch (don't skip this)

- Promote the downsell / "you missed it" offer (Product 4).
- Deliver bonuses promptly; ask buyers for testimonials.
- Debrief: clicks, conversion, EPC, best asset (Product 24).
- Save winning swipes for the next launch.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Pick a launch with good commissions + a leaderboard (Product 20 to vet it).
2. Fill in the day-by-day timeline (`launch-timeline.md`).
3. Pre-warm, hit hard on open + close, then debrief.
