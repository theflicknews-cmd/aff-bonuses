## Phase 1 — Pre-launch (7-14 days before)

- [ ] Get approved on the affiliate/JV page
- [ ] Save the vendor's assets + key dates
- [ ] Score the offer (Product 20)
- [ ] Build my bonus stack + bonus page (Products 2, 25)
- [ ] Write/schedule launch emails (Product 4)
- [ ] Create teaser content (no pitch) for the problem/topic
- [ ] Warm my list: "something good is coming"
- [ ] Prepare review/demo video (Product 7)

## Phase 2 — Open cart (launch day)

- [ ] Send "it's live" email at peak open time
- [ ] Post launch announcement on all channels
- [ ] Publish review/demo video
- [ ] Run Stories selling sequence (Product 26)
- [ ] Pin link everywhere (bio, pinned post/comment)
- [ ] Add a fast-action bonus for early buyers
- [ ] Reply to all comments/DMs quickly
- [ ] (Optional) 2nd email to non-openers in the evening

## Phase 3 — Mid-launch (days 2-4)

- [ ] Day 2: value/demo email + post
- [ ] Day 3: social proof email + testimonials
- [ ] Day 4: objection-handling email + content
- [ ] Keep posting daily; share early results/screenshots
- [ ] Retarget bonus-page visitors who didn't buy (Product 13)

## Phase 4 — Cart close (final 24-48h)

- [ ] "Closing soon" email (final 24h)
- [ ] Bonus-deadline reminder (final hours)
- [ ] "Last call" email (final 1-3h)
- [ ] Final-hours posts/Stories with countdown
- [ ] Reply to last-minute questions fast

## Phase 5 — Post-launch

- [ ] Promote downsell / "you missed it" offer
- [ ] Deliver all bonuses promptly
- [ ] Thank buyers; request testimonials
- [ ] Debrief: clicks, conv %, EPC, best asset (Product 24)
- [ ] Save winning swipes for next time
