## The 4 things all persuasive copy does

1. **Grabs attention** (a hook that stops them).
2. **Speaks to a real desire/pain** (it's about them, not you).
3. **Builds belief** (proof, logic, story).
4. **Drives one action** (a clear, single CTA).

The formulas below are just reliable ways to hit all four.

## The one rule that beats fancy copy

**Clarity > cleverness.** Confused people don't buy. Write like you talk, to one
person, about one thing they want. Then add a formula for structure.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Pick a formula matching your goal (table above).
2. Fill the template with your offer's pain, promise, and proof.
3. Read it out loud — cut anything that sounds stiff.
4. End with ONE clear CTA to your bonus page / link.
