# 15 Copywriting Formulas

Replace `[PRODUCT]`, `[PAIN]`, `[BENEFIT]`, `[RESULT]`. Each has a structure + a mini example.

---

**1. AIDA — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action**
- A: Hook. I: build interest. D: stoke desire (benefits/proof). A: CTA.
- *Ex:* "Tired of `[PAIN]`? (A) Here's a faster way... (I) `[PRODUCT]` helps you
  `[BENEFIT]` (D). Grab it + my bonuses here (A)."

**2. PAS — Problem, Agitate, Solution**
- State problem -> agitate the cost of it -> present the solution.
- *Ex:* "You're losing sales to other affiliates (P). Every promo you do this
  way leaves money on the table (A). `[PRODUCT]` fixes that (S)."

**3. BAB — Before, After, Bridge**
- Before (their pain) -> After (the dream) -> Bridge (the product).
- *Ex:* "Right now `[before]`. Imagine `[after]`. `[PRODUCT]` is the bridge."

**4. PASTOR — Problem, Amplify, Story, Transformation, Offer, Response**
- Great for long-form sales pages and emails. Story + proof do the heavy lifting.

**5. FAB — Features, Advantages, Benefits**
- Turn each feature into what it does and what it means *for them*.
- *Ex:* "It auto-generates content (F), so you save hours (A), meaning you post
  daily without burnout (B)."

**6. The 4 U's — Useful, Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific**
- A headline checklist. Hit as many as possible.
- *Ex:* "Get 30 days of content in one afternoon (useful, specific) - before the
  bonus closes Friday (urgent)."

**7. APP — Agree, Promise, Preview**
- Agree with a belief -> promise a solution -> preview what's coming.
- *Ex:* "Posting daily is exhausting (agree). There's a shortcut (promise). Here's
  exactly how (preview)."

**8. PPPP — Picture, Promise, Prove, Push**
- Paint a picture -> make a promise -> prove it -> push to act.

**9. Star-Story-Solution**
- Star (relatable hero) -> Story (struggle + turning point) -> Solution (product).
- Perfect for emails and VSLs (Product 7).

**10. QUEST — Qualify, Understand, Educate, Stimulate, Transition**
- Qualify the reader -> show you understand -> educate -> stimulate desire ->
  transition to the offer.

**11. The 4 P's — Promise, Picture, Proof, Push**
- Similar to PPPP; lead with the promise.

**12. AIDCA — AIDA + Conviction**
- Add a "Conviction" step (proof/guarantee) before the action. Great when trust is low.

**13. SLAP — Stop, Look, Act, Purchase**
- For short-form/ads: Stop the scroll, get them to Look, prompt an Act, lead to Purchase.

**14. "How-To"**
- "How to `[RESULT]` without `[PAIN]`." Simple, evergreen, high-converting headline.
- *Ex:* "How to get your first affiliate sale without an audience."

**15. The 5 Objections (close the gaps)**
- Answer these in your copy: "No time / No money / Won't work for me / Don't believe
  you / Don't need it now." Address each = remove the reason not to buy.

## Quick-fill PAS template (the workhorse)

> **Problem:** Struggling with `[PAIN]`?
> **Agitate:** It's costing you `[consequence]` - and it won't fix itself.
> **Solution:** `[PRODUCT]` helps you `[BENEFIT]` so you can `[RESULT]`.
> **CTA:** Get it (+ my bonuses) here -> `[LINK]`

---

*Pick a formula, fill it in, read it aloud, cut the fluff, one CTA. That's pro copy.*
