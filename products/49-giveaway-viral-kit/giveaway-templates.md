# Giveaway Templates

Replace `[PRIZE]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[NICHE]`, `[DATES]`, `[#]`. Follow platform rules.

## Entry page copy

> **🎁 Win `[PRIZE]`! (Free to Enter)**
> I'm giving away `[PRIZE]` to one lucky `[audience]`. Enter with your email below -
> then share your link to earn bonus entries (more shares = more chances to win).
> Ends `[DATES]`.
> **[Enter the Giveaway]**

## Prize ideas (niche-specific = future buyers)

- A bundle of my bonus products (this vault) + `[PRODUCT]`
- 1 year of `[the tool you promote]`
- A 1:1 strategy call + my templates
- A `[niche]`-relevant gift card or gear

## Referral incentive copy (shown after entry)

> 🎉 You're in! Want to boost your odds?
> Share your unique link: `[their link]`
> - Refer 1 friend = `[#]` bonus entries
> - **Refer 3 friends** = unlock `[instant bonus]` (yours to keep, win or not!)
> - Top referrer wins a guaranteed `[prize]`

## Promotion swipes

**Email to your list:**
> Subject: I'm giving away `[PRIZE]` 🎁
> Quick one - I'm running a free giveaway for `[PRIZE]`. Enter here (takes 10
> seconds): `[link]`. Share with friends to boost your chances. Ends `[DATES]`.

**Social caption:**
> 🎁 GIVEAWAY: I'm giving one `[audience]` a `[PRIZE]`! To enter: `[steps]`. Tag a
> friend who needs this 👇 Ends `[DATES]`. (Link in bio to enter + earn bonus entries.)

## Sample rules block (adapt + verify locally)

> **Giveaway Rules:** Open to `[eligibility/region]`, 18+. Runs `[start]` to `[end]`.
> Winner(s) selected `[method - e.g., random draw from entries]` and announced
> `[when/where]`. Winner notified by email; must respond within `[#]` days. No
> purchase necessary to enter. This giveaway is run by `[YOUR NAME]` and is not
> sponsored or endorsed by any platform. `[Affiliate disclosure if applicable.]`

## Consolation offer (to all non-winners)

**Email subject:** The giveaway's over - but here's something for you
> Congrats to `[winner]`! Didn't win? I didn't want you to leave empty-handed. For
> the next 48 hours, grab `[PRODUCT]` through my link and I'll include `[BONUS]`
> free: `[LINK]`. Thanks for entering 🙏

## Winner announcement (social proof)

> 🎉 We have a winner! Congrats `[winner]` on winning `[PRIZE]`! Thank you to the
> `[#]` people who entered - I've got something for you too (check your inbox).
> More giveaways coming - follow so you don't miss the next one.

---

## Setup checklist

- [ ] Pick a niche-specific prize
- [ ] Choose a giveaway tool with referral tracking
- [ ] Build the entry page + referral rewards
- [ ] Write the rules + disclosures
- [ ] Prepare the consolation offer + nurture sequence (Product 4)
- [ ] Promote across all channels
- [ ] Announce winners publicly + follow up with everyone

---

*A giveaway grows the list; the follow-up grows the income. Niche-specific prize +
consolation offer = entrants who actually buy.*
