## The 3 numbers every affiliate should know

- **EPC (Earnings Per Click):** avg $ earned per click you send. Your north star.
- **Conversion rate:** % of clicks that buy.
- **EPL (Earnings Per Lead):** avg $ earned per email subscriber over time.
