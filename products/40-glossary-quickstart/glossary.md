# Affiliate Marketing Glossary (60+ Terms)

Plain-English definitions of the terms you'll actually run into.

## Core terms
- **Affiliate:** you - the person promoting a product for a commission.
- **Vendor / Merchant:** the product owner who pays the commission.
- **Affiliate network:** platform that tracks sales and pays you (WarriorPlus, JVZoo, LaunchpadJV).
- **Affiliate link / Hoplink:** your unique tracked link.
- **Commission:** your cut of a sale.
- **Recurring commission:** ongoing payments for subscription products.
- **Cookie:** data that tracks a buyer to you for a set window.
- **Cookie duration:** how long after a click you still get credit.
- **Conversion:** when a click turns into a sale (or lead).
- **Niche:** the specific market/topic you focus on.

## Money & metrics
- **EPC (Earnings Per Click):** average earnings per click you send.
- **CR (Conversion Rate):** % of clicks that buy.
- **EPL (Earnings Per Lead):** avg earnings per email subscriber.
- **AOV (Average Order Value):** average total spend per buyer (incl. upsells).
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** % who click your link/content.
- **ROAS (Return On Ad Spend):** revenue / ad spend.
- **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition):** what it costs to get a sale/lead.
- **CPL (Cost Per Lead):** what it costs to get a subscriber.
- **Gravity (some networks):** popularity/sales indicator of an offer.
- **Refund / chargeback rate:** % of sales returned/disputed.

## Offers & funnels
- **Front-end (FE):** the first/main product in a funnel.
- **Upsell (OTO - One-Time Offer):** an add-on offered after the FE.
- **Downsell:** a cheaper alternative if they decline the upsell.
- **Funnel:** the sequence of pages/offers a buyer moves through.
- **Squeeze / opt-in page:** a page built to capture emails.
- **Bridge page:** a page that pre-frames an offer before the sales page.
- **Sales page / VSL:** the page/video that sells the product.
- **Tripwire:** a low-priced offer to convert leads into buyers.
- **Order bump:** a small add-on offered on the checkout page.

## Bonuses & promotion
- **Bonus / bonus stack:** extras you add to win the sale.
- **Bonus page:** your page showcasing your bonus stack.
- **Swipe / swipe file:** ready-made copy you can adapt.
- **Launch:** a coordinated promotion around a product's release.
- **JV (Joint Venture):** partnering with the vendor/others to promote.
- **Leaderboard:** ranking of top affiliates in a launch (often with prizes).
- **Dime sale:** price rises as more units sell (creates urgency).
- **Open/close cart:** when an offer becomes available/unavailable.

## Traffic & list
- **Organic traffic:** free traffic (SEO, social, video).
- **Paid traffic:** ads you pay for.
- **Retargeting:** ads shown to people who already visited.
- **Lead magnet:** free resource given in exchange for an email.
- **Autoresponder:** email tool that sends automated sequences.
- **Broadcast:** a one-off email to your list.
- **Segmentation:** grouping subscribers by interest/behavior.
- **Open rate / click rate:** % who open / click your emails.
- **Deliverability:** whether your emails reach the inbox.
- **List:** your collection of email subscribers.

## Content & SEO
- **Pillar content:** a core, comprehensive piece you repurpose.
- **Repurposing:** turning one piece into many formats.
- **Keyword:** the search term you target.
- **Buyer intent:** how close a searcher is to purchasing.
- **Backlink:** a link from another site to yours (helps SEO).
- **CTA (Call To Action):** the action you want them to take.
- **Above the fold:** what's visible before scrolling.

## Compliance & misc
- **Disclosure:** stating you may earn a commission (required).
- **FTC:** US agency whose guidelines require affiliate disclosure.
- **Income disclaimer:** statement that results aren't guaranteed.
- **PLR (Private Label Rights):** content you can edit/rebrand (like this vault).
- **MRR/RR (Master/Resell Rights):** rights to resell a product.
- **Affiliate manager:** the vendor's contact for affiliates.
- **Net-30 / instant payout:** when/how you get paid.
- **Cloaked link:** a tidy, branded version of your affiliate link.
- **Split test (A/B test):** comparing two versions to see which wins.
- **Evergreen:** content/funnels that work continuously over time.
