# Product 4 — Affiliate Promo Email Swipe File

**Niche:** Affiliate Marketing / Email
**Format:** 12 fill-in-the-blank email swipes
**Perceived value:** $77

The money in affiliate marketing is in the follow-up. This swipe file gives
buyers **12 proven promo emails** that map to a real launch arc — from "soft
intro" to "last call" — so they can promote any WarriorPlus/JVZoo offer without
writing from scratch.

## What's inside

- [`emails.md`](emails.md) — all 12 swipes with subject lines, body, and CTA.

## The 5-day launch sequence (core 5 swipes)

| Email | When | Job |
|-------|------|-----|
| 1 — Soft intro | Day 1 | Tease the problem + that a solution is coming |
| 2 — Value/demo | Day 2 | Teach something + show the product solving it |
| 3 — Social proof | Day 3 | Results, testimonials, your own use |
| 4 — Objections | Day 4 | Knock down the top reasons not to buy |
| 5 — Last call | Day 5 | Deadline + bonus urgency |

The remaining **7 swipes** cover: review email, comparison email, bonus-reveal
email, cart-open, cart-close, downsell/"missed it", and a re-engagement email.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Open `emails.md`.
2. Find/replace `[PRODUCT]`, `[BENEFIT]`, `[LINK]`, `[DEADLINE]`, `[YOUR NAME]`.
3. Adjust the tone to match your voice (don't send them word-for-word as a list).
4. Schedule them around the offer's open/close dates.
5. Link to your bonus page (Product 2) for max conversions.

> Deliverability tip: keep links to 1–2 per email, avoid spam-trigger words in
> subject lines, and always honor the offer's promo/spam rules.
