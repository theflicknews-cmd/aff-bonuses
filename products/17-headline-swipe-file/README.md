## The headline picking rule

Match the headline to the **temperature** of your reader:

- **Cold** (don't know you): lead with curiosity or a bold benefit.
- **Warm** (on your list): lead with value or a specific result.
- **Hot** (ready to buy): lead with urgency, the offer, or social proof.

## The 4 ingredients of a high-converting headline

1. **Specificity** — "$1,247 in 9 days" beats "make money."
2. **Self-interest** — what's in it for them.
3. **Curiosity** — an open loop they need closed.
4. **Believability** — don't overpromise; credibility converts.
