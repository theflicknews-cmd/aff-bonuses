# 25 DM & Outreach Scripts

Personalize the first line every time. Replace `[NAME]`, `[FREEBIE]`, `[PRODUCT]`,
`[NICHE]`, `[TOPIC]`.

## Inbound (they engaged first)

**1. Keyword comment reply**
> Hey `[NAME]`! Thanks for commenting - here's the `[FREEBIE]` I mentioned: `[link]`.
> What are you working on in `[NICHE]` right now?

**2. They asked a public question**
> Saw your question about `[TOPIC]` - great one. Short answer: `[tip]`. Happy to go
> deeper if useful.

**3. New follower welcome**
> Thanks for the follow, `[NAME]`! Quick q so I can post useful stuff - what's your
> biggest challenge with `[NICHE]` right now?

**4. They liked several posts**
> Hey `[NAME]`, noticed you've been checking out my `[TOPIC]` posts 🙂 Anything
> specific you're trying to figure out? Happy to point you in the right direction.

**5. They downloaded your freebie**
> Hope the `[FREEBIE]` is helpful, `[NAME]`! Did you get a chance to try `[step]`
> yet? Let me know if you hit any snags.

## Value-first openers (warm audience)

**6. Compliment + question**
> Love what you're doing with `[their thing]`, `[NAME]`. How's `[goal]` going for you?

**7. Helpful tip, no ask**
> Hey `[NAME]`, saw your post on `[TOPIC]` - one thing that helped me a ton was
> `[tip]`. Thought it might be useful!

**8. Shared resource**
> `[NAME]`, this reminded me of you - `[resource/tip]`. No agenda, just thought
> you'd find it handy.

## The soft transition to the offer

**9. After rapport**
> Since you're working on `[goal]`, the thing that made the biggest difference for me
> was `[PRODUCT]`. Want me to send the link? I'll include a bonus if you grab it
> through me.

**10. Answering "what do you use?"**
> For `[task]` I use `[PRODUCT]` - it `[benefit]`. Here's the link (+ my bonuses):
> `[link]`. Happy to help you set it up.

**11. Problem -> solution**
> You mentioned `[problem]` - that's exactly what `[PRODUCT]` fixed for me. Want the
> details?

## Follow-ups

**12. Clicked but didn't buy**
> Hey `[NAME]`, did you have any questions about `[PRODUCT]`? Happy to help - and the
> bonus offer ends `[deadline]`.

**13. Gentle nudge**
> No pressure at all `[NAME]` - just wanted to make sure you saw the bonuses close
> soon. Here's the link again: `[link]`.

**14. Re-engage cold lead**
> Hey `[NAME]`, it's been a while! Still working on `[goal]`? I just put together
> something that might help: `[freebie/link]`.

**15. Value follow-up (no pitch)**
> Thought of you - here's a quick tip on `[TOPIC]`: `[tip]`. How's it going?

## Objection handling in DMs

**16. "Is it worth it?"**
> Honest answer: if you want `[result]`, yes - here's why `[reason]`. And it's backed
> by `[guarantee]`, so low risk.

**17. "I'm not sure it's for me."**
> Totally fair. It's best for `[fit]`. Does that sound like you? If not, I'll be
> straight and say so.

**18. "Too expensive."**
> I hear you. There's `[payment option]`, and honestly the bigger cost is staying
> stuck with `[problem]`. Want me to break down what you get?

## Networking / collab outreach

**19. Compliment + collab idea**
> Hey `[NAME]`, big fan of your `[content]`. I work with a similar audience in
> `[NICHE]` - open to a value swap (shoutout/collab)? Could help us both.

**20. Offer value first**
> `[NAME]`, I'd love to feature your `[thing]` to my audience - no strings. If it
> goes well, maybe we explore a cross-promo?

**21. JV partner intro**
> Hi `[NAME]`, I promote offers in `[NICHE]` and have `[audience size/asset]`. Would
> you be open to a quick chat about promoting `[their offer]` together?

## Re-engagement & retention

**22. Buyer check-in**
> Hey `[NAME]`, how's `[PRODUCT]` working out? Anything I can help with? (Also grab
> your bonuses here if you haven't: `[link]`.)

**23. Ask for a testimonial**
> So glad `[PRODUCT]` is working for you! Would you mind sharing a quick line about
> your experience? It really helps others decide.

**24. Win-back**
> Miss having you around, `[NAME]`! Here's a fresh `[freebie]` to say thanks for
> being here: `[link]`.

**25. Referral ask**
> `[NAME]`, know anyone else struggling with `[problem]`? Feel free to send them my
> `[freebie]` - happy to help them too: `[link]`.

---

*Conversation first, link second. Be the helpful person and the sales follow.*
