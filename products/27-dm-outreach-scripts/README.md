## The golden rule of DMs

**Start a conversation, not a pitch.** Lead with genuine interest or value. The
pitch comes only after you've built a little rapport and earned the right. Mass
copy-paste pitching gets you ignored, reported, and banned.

## The value-first DM framework (CARE)

1. **C — Connect:** reference something real (their post, comment, profile).
2. **A — Ask:** a genuine question about their situation/goal.
3. **R — Relate/Reveal value:** share a tip or resource that helps them.
4. **E — Easy invite:** a low-pressure offer ("want the link/resource?").

## When to use DMs in affiliate marketing

- Someone comments your keyword on a post.
- A follower asks a question publicly.
- Re-engaging a warm lead who downloaded your freebie.
- Following up with people who clicked but didn't buy.
- Networking with potential collab/JV partners.

## Rules to stay safe + effective

- Never lead with a raw affiliate link.
- Personalize the first line — no obvious copy-paste.
- Give before you ask.
- Respect "no" — don't badger.
- Don't mass-DM cold strangers (it's spam and gets you banned).

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Match the scenario to a script in `scripts.md`.
2. Personalize the opener (always).
3. Lead with value; invite, don't pressure.
4. Send the link only once there's genuine interest.
