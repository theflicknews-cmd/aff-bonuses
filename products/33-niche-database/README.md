## How to evaluate any niche (quick filter)

A niche is worth pursuing if it has:
1. **Buyers** — people actively spending (not just interested).
2. **Offers** — affiliate products you can promote (and stand behind).
3. **Content angles** — enough to post consistently for a year.
4. **Personal fit** — you can tolerate (ideally enjoy) the topic.

Pair this with Product 20 (Offer Scorecard) to validate specific offers.

## Why some niches pay better than others

- **Recurring offers** (software/subscriptions) = monthly income.
- **High-ticket** (coaching, courses) = bigger commissions per sale.
- **Evergreen demand** (health, wealth, relationships) = stable, year-round buyers.
- **"Hungry" buyers** (urgent, painful problems) convert faster.
