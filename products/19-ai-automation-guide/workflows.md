# Automation Recipes

Each recipe = Trigger -> Steps -> Result. Build in Zapier/Make/n8n. Tool names are
examples; use your equivalents.

---

## Recipe 1 — Lead Capture -> Welcome
**Trigger:** New opt-in form submission.
**Steps:**
1. Add subscriber to your email tool with tag `[lead-magnet-name]`.
2. Send the delivery email (with the lead magnet link).
3. Add a row to a Google Sheet for tracking.
**Result:** Instant delivery + organized list, zero manual work.

## Recipe 2 — AI Content Drafting
**Trigger:** New row in a "Content Ideas" sheet.
**Steps:**
1. Send the topic to an AI step with a prompt (e.g., Product 8's prompts).
2. Receive the draft (email/post/caption).
3. Save the draft back to the sheet or a "Drafts" doc.
**Result:** A queue of drafts ready for you to edit and approve.

## Recipe 3 — Social Scheduling
**Trigger:** Approved content in your sheet/doc.
**Steps:**
1. Push the content to your scheduler (Buffer/Publer/native).
2. Set the post time per platform.
**Result:** A week of content scheduled in minutes.

## Recipe 4 — New DM/Comment -> Route
**Trigger:** New comment/DM with a keyword (e.g., "INFO").
**Steps:**
1. AI step classifies intent (question / lead / spam).
2. If lead: send a templated reply with your link.
3. Log the contact in your sheet.
**Result:** Warm leads handled instantly, even while you sleep.

## Recipe 5 — Content Repurposing
**Trigger:** New pillar piece (blog/transcript) added.
**Steps:**
1. AI splits it into email, thread, carousel, and 3 video hooks (Product 8).
2. Save each output to your content queue.
**Result:** One piece becomes a week of content automatically.

## Recipe 6 — Lead Scoring / Segmenting
**Trigger:** Subscriber clicks a link / opens X emails.
**Steps:**
1. Add/adjust a score field in your email tool.
2. Tag "hot" leads above a threshold.
**Result:** Promote to your warmest leads first = higher conversions.

## Recipe 7 — Sale -> Bonus Delivery
**Trigger:** Purchase notification (network/email/webhook).
**Steps:**
1. Verify the buyer (receipt/affiliate ID).
2. Send the bonus download links automatically.
3. Tag them as "buyer" for future offers.
**Result:** Bonuses delivered instantly = fewer refunds, happier buyers.

## Recipe 8 — Mention Monitoring
**Trigger:** New mention of your niche keyword (RSS/Google Alerts/social).
**Steps:**
1. AI summarizes the mention.
2. Send yourself (or a channel) a digest.
**Result:** Stay on top of trends and engagement opportunities.

## Recipe 9 — Weekly Stats Report
**Trigger:** Scheduled (every Monday).
**Steps:**
1. Pull clicks/sales/opt-ins from your tools or sheet.
2. AI writes a short summary + suggests one focus for the week.
3. Email it to yourself.
**Result:** A standing dashboard without manual reporting.

## Recipe 10 — AI Research Assistant
**Trigger:** You add an offer/competitor URL to a sheet.
**Steps:**
1. AI summarizes the offer: benefits, audience, objections, angle ideas.
2. Save the summary next to the URL.
**Result:** Faster offer vetting (pairs with Product 20).
