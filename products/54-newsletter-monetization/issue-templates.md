# Newsletter Issue Templates + Calendar

Replace `[NICHE]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[LINK]`. Keep a consistent structure so readers
know what to expect.

## Reusable header/intro

> **`[Newsletter Name]` — Issue #`[#]`**
> Hey `[FIRST NAME]`, `[1-2 personal/relevant lines]`. Here's what's worth your time
> this week 👇

## Reusable sign-off

> That's it for this week. Hit reply and tell me what you're working on - I read
> every email.
> — `[YOUR NAME]`
> *P.S. Some links are affiliate links; I only recommend what I'd use myself.*

---

## Format 1 — Curated Roundup

> **3 things worth your attention in `[NICHE]`:**
> 1. `[Link/news]` - why it matters: `[1 line]`
> 2. `[Link/resource]` - `[1 line]`
> 3. `[Link/tool]` - `[1 line]`
>
> **🛠 Tool of the week:** `[PRODUCT]` - `[what it does + benefit]`. I put together a
> bonus stack if you grab it through me: `[LINK]`
>
> `[Sign-off]`

## Format 2 — Single-Topic Teach

> **This week: `[topic]`**
> `[The lesson - 3-5 short paragraphs of genuine value.]`
>
> **Make it easier:** the tool I use for this is `[PRODUCT]` -> `[LINK]` (+ bonus).
>
> `[Sign-off]`

## Format 3 — Tips + Tool

> **`[#]` quick `[NICHE]` tips:**
> - `[tip]`
> - `[tip]`
> - `[tip]`
>
> **Recommended:** `[PRODUCT]` for `[use case]` -> `[LINK]`
>
> `[Sign-off]`

## Format 4 — Deep Dive (occasional)

> **Deep dive: Is `[PRODUCT]` worth it?**
> `[Honest, thorough review: what it is, who it's for, pros/cons, verdict.]`
> Get it + my bonuses: `[LINK]`. (Honest review - affiliate link.)
>
> `[Sign-off]`
