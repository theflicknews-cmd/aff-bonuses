# Community Post & Reply Templates

Replace `[NICHE]`, `[RESULT]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[FREEBIE]`.

## Daily post templates (for your own group)

**Value post**
> 💡 Quick `[NICHE]` tip: `[tip]`. Try it this week and tell me how it goes 👇

**Engagement question**
> Be honest - what's your biggest struggle with `[TOPIC]` right now? Drop it below
> and I'll reply with a suggestion.

**Poll**
> Which do you want next: `[Option A]` or `[Option B]`? Vote 👇

**Win celebration**
> 🎉 Shoutout to `[member]` for `[win]`! Who else made progress this week?

**Story/lesson**
> The biggest mistake I made early in `[NICHE]`: `[mistake]`. Here's what I'd do
> differently - `[lesson]`.

**Soft promo (spaced out)**
> I get asked what I use for `[task]` all the time. Honest answer: `[PRODUCT]`.
> Here's why -> `[1-2 benefits]`. If you grab it through my link this week I'll add
> `[BONUS]`. Comment "INFO" and I'll send details.

## Weekly ritual ideas (build habit + engagement)

- **Mon:** Goal-setting thread ("What are you working on this week?")
- **Wed:** Win Wednesday ("Share a win, big or small")
- **Fri:** Q&A / "Ask me anything about `[NICHE]`"
- **Sun:** Resource drop (link to your content/freebie)

## Reply templates (for others' groups - value first)

**Answering a question**
> Great question! Here's what's worked for me: `[genuine, specific answer]`. Happy
> to share more if helpful.

**Offering a resource (no spammy link)**
> I actually put together a free `[FREEBIE]` on exactly this - happy to send it over
> if you want, just let me know 🙂

**Building rapport with an admin**
> Love this group - the discussions on `[topic]` are gold. Thanks for running it!

## Entry questions for your own group (capture + qualify)

1. What's your #1 goal with `[NICHE]` right now?
2. What's your biggest obstacle?
3. (Optional) Drop your best email to get the free `[FREEBIE]` -> `[link]`

---

*Value first, promote second. A warm community converts better than any cold traffic.*
