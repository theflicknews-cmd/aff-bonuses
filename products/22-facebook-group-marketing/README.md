# Product 22 — Facebook Group & Community Marketing Playbook

**Niche:** Social Media / Community
**Format:** Strategy + engagement templates
**Perceived value:** $57

A warm community is the highest-converting "audience" in affiliate marketing —
members trust your recommendations. This playbook shows buyers how to grow and
leverage Facebook Groups (and any community) to drive affiliate sales the right way.

## What's inside

- This guide — the two strategies (your own group vs others' groups)
- [`engagement-templates.md`](engagement-templates.md) — post + reply templates

## Two strategies

### Strategy A — Build your own group (highest long-term value)
You own the audience, set the rules, and can promote freely (within FB's terms).
Slower to start, but it becomes a powerful, warm sales channel.

### Strategy B — Add value in others' groups (fastest start)
Find groups where your audience hangs out, become genuinely helpful, and let people
find you. **Never spam links** — lead with value; let your profile/answers pull them in.

## Building your own group (the system)

1. **Niche it down:** "`[Audience]` who want `[outcome]`" — specific beats broad.
2. **Set 3 entry questions** (capture emails / qualify members).
3. **Post daily value** using the content mix (below).
4. **Run weekly rituals:** Motivation Monday, Win Wednesday, Q&A Friday, etc.
5. **Promote periodically:** ~1 in 5 posts points to an offer/bonus page.
6. **Funnel to email:** move members onto your list (you don't own the group).

## The content mix (keep it engaging)

| Type | % | Example |
|------|---|---------|
| Value/teaching | 40% | Tips, tutorials, mini-lessons |
| Engagement | 30% | Questions, polls, wins |
| Story/personal | 15% | Behind-the-scenes, lessons |
| Promo | 15% | Offer + bonus (spaced out) |

## Leveraging others' groups (without getting banned)

- Read and follow each group's rules (many ban links).
- Answer questions thoroughly — be the most helpful person in the thread.
- Optimize your profile so it sells for you (clear bio + link).
- Offer a free resource via DM or comment ("happy to share my checklist").
- Build relationships with admins — they can become collab partners.

## Compliance

- Follow Facebook's terms + each group's rules.
- Disclose affiliate relationships.
- Don't spam, don't fake engagement, don't mass-DM.

## How to use (for the affiliate)

1. Pick Strategy A, B, or both.
2. For A: launch a niched group with entry questions -> email capture.
3. For B: list 5 active groups and start adding value daily.
4. Use the templates to post and engage consistently.
