# Affiliate Bonus Vault

Ready-to-use digital products you can attach as **bonuses** to any promotion on
WarriorPlus, JVZoo, or LaunchpadJV. Each product is self-contained, niche-relevant
(affiliate marketing / AI / social media), and written to be repackaged, rebranded,
and delivered to buyers immediately.

## How to use these bonuses

1. Pick the products that match the offer you're promoting.
2. (Optional) Rebrand — swap `[Your Name]`, `[Your Brand]`, and links for yours.
3. Export to PDF (for guides/checklists) or upload the HTML (for the bonus page).
4. Deliver via your autoresponder, a download page, or your WarriorPlus/JVZoo
   "Deliver Bonus" feature.
5. Announce the bonus in your promo emails and on the bonus page.

> Tip: Bonuses convert best when they remove the *next* obstacle the buyer hits
> right after purchasing the front-end offer. Stack 3–5 of these per promo.
