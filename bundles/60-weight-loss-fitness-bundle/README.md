## The weight-loss/fitness buyer avatar (pre-filled)

- **Who:** people who want to lose weight, get fit, or feel better - often busy,
  frustrated, and tired of yo-yo attempts.
- **They want:** to feel confident, healthy, and energetic; a method that fits their life.
- **They fear:** failing again, restrictive diets, wasting money, judgment, "it won't
  work for my body."
- **Already tried:** fad diets, gym memberships they didn't use, programs they quit.
- **Their words:** "I've tried everything," "I don't have time," "I always start and
  stop," "nothing works for me."
- **Buying triggers:** relatability, simplicity, sustainability ("not another crash
  diet"), real (responsible) testimonials, support.

## Top angles for weight-loss/fitness offers (pre-built)

1. **Sustainable, not extreme:** "No crash diets or hours in the gym."
2. **Busy-friendly:** "For people who don't have time."
3. **Relatable empathy:** "If you've tried everything, this is different because..."
4. **Simplicity:** "A simple plan you can actually stick to."
5. **Feel-good (beyond the scale):** energy, confidence, health.
6. **Support/community:** "You're not doing this alone."
