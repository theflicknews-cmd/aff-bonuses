## The finance buyer avatar (pre-filled)

- **Who:** people who feel behind financially, want control of their money, or want to
  start investing/earning more.
- **They want:** security, control, to stop living paycheck-to-paycheck, to build wealth.
- **They fear:** losing money, complexity, being "bad with money," scams, being too late.
- **Already tried:** budgeting apps they abandoned, intimidating investing info.
- **Their words:** "I'm bad with money," "investing is confusing," "I don't earn
  enough to save," "is it too late for me?"
- **Buying triggers:** simplicity, a clear first step, relatability, education over
  hype, low risk.

## Top angles (pre-built)

1. **Simplicity:** "Personal finance made simple - no jargon."
2. **Control:** "Finally take control of your money."
3. **Beginner-safe:** "Start investing/saving even if you feel behind."
4. **Education over hype:** "Learn the fundamentals (not get-rich nonsense)."
5. **Small-steps:** "Start with what you have, today."
