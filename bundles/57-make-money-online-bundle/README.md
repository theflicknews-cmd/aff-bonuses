## The MMO-buyer avatar (pre-filled)

- **Who:** people seeking extra/online income — side-hustlers, beginners, "shiny
  object" buyers, and frustrated past buyers.
- **They want:** more income, freedom/time, a system that "finally works," hope.
- **They fear:** being scammed again, complexity, wasting money, failing once more.
- **Already tried:** other courses/tools that didn't deliver (they're skeptical).
- **Their words:** "does this actually work?", "I've bought stuff before," "I don't
  have much to invest," "I need this to be simple."
- **Buying triggers:** believable proof, simplicity, low risk (guarantee), a
  trustworthy guide, and a clear first step.

## Top angles for MMO offers (pre-built)

1. **Simplicity:** "Finally, a system simple enough to actually follow."
2. **Beginner-safe:** "No experience, audience, or tech skills needed."
3. **Anti-shiny-object:** "Stop buying course after course. This is the last one you need."
