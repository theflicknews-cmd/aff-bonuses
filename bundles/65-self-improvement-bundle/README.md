## The self-improvement buyer avatar (pre-filled)

- **Who:** people who feel stuck, scattered, or unfulfilled and want to become more
  focused, disciplined, confident, or productive.
- **They want:** to follow through, build better habits, feel in control, reach goals.
- **They fear:** staying stuck, being "lazy," wasting potential, starting and quitting again.
- **Already tried:** apps, planners, motivation videos that wore off.
- **Their words:** "I keep starting and stopping," "I procrastinate," "I'm
  unmotivated," "I know what to do but don't do it."
- **Buying triggers:** a system (not just motivation), relatability, quick wins,
  identity ("become the person who...").
