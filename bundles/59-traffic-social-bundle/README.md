## The traffic/social buyer avatar (pre-filled)

- **Who:** creators, marketers, and small-biz owners struggling to get seen, grow, or
  drive clicks.
- **They want:** more views, followers, traffic, and ultimately clicks/sales.
- **They fear:** posting into the void, wasting time, "the algorithm hates me,"
  inconsistency.
- **Already tried:** posting randomly, buying followers, giving up after low reach.
- **Their words:** "no one sees my stuff," "I don't have time to post," "I don't know
  what to post," "my reach is dead."

  consistently without the grind."
