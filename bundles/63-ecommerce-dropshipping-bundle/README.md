## The ecom/dropshipping buyer avatar (pre-filled)

- **Who:** aspiring store owners and side-hustlers drawn to selling products online
  without holding inventory.
- **They want:** a profitable store, product-picking confidence, sales without a warehouse.
- **They fear:** picking losing products, ad money wasted, complex logistics, scams.
- **Already tried:** maybe a store that got no sales, watching ecom gurus on YouTube.
- **Their words:** "what do I sell?", "I don't want to hold inventory," "ads are
  confusing," "is dropshipping dead?"
- **Buying triggers:** product-research help, proof of stores that work, low startup
  risk, step-by-step setup.
