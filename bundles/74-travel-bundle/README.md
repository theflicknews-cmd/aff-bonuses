## The travel buyer avatar (pre-filled)

- **Who:** people who love to travel (or dream of it) and want to do it more often,
  cheaper, and with less hassle.
- **They want:** more trips, lower costs, unforgettable experiences, stress-free planning.
- **They fear:** overpaying, tourist traps, travel stress, missing out, "I can't afford
  to travel."
- **Already tried:** booking the expensive way, overwhelming planning, generic advice.
- **Their words:** "travel is so expensive," "I don't know how people afford it," "I
  hate planning," "I want to travel more."
- **Buying triggers:** save money (points/deals), insider tips, wanderlust/inspiration,
  ease of planning.
