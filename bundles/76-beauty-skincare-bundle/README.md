## The beauty/skincare buyer avatar (pre-filled)

- **Who:** people (often women, but not only) who want to look and feel their best,
  fix a specific concern, or simplify their routine.
- **They want:** clear/glowing skin, confidence, to look youthful/rested, a routine
  that works.
- **They fear:** wasting money on products that don't work, irritation, "nothing works
  for my skin," looking older.
- **Already tried:** drugstore products, conflicting routines, expensive things that
  flopped.
- **Their words:** "my skin is `[concern]`," "I've tried everything," "I want a simple
  routine," "is this worth it?"
- **Buying triggers:** visible results, simplicity, relatability, ingredient
  transparency, confidence boost.

## Top angles (pre-built)

1. **Fix the concern:** "Finally tackle `[acne/dryness/aging]`."
