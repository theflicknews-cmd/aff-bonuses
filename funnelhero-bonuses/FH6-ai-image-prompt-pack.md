## Pro tips for designed-not-templated results
- **Add your text later** — generators are weak at text; place headlines in FunnelHero's
  editor over the image.
- **Always set aspect ratio** to fit the slot (16:9 hero, 1:1 cards/icons, 2:3 covers, 9:16
  mobile/Stories).
- **Ask for "space for text overlay"** on hero/banner shots.
- **Match your brand colors** by naming the hex/colors in the prompt.
- **Generate 2-3 variations** and pick the strongest.
