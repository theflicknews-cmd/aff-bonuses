## Which brain, and why (quick guide)

- **Opus** — deep, nuanced, long-form. Best for strategy, full sales pages, story/VSL
  copy, and anything needing persuasion + subtlety.
- **Sonnet** — fast, structured, reliable. Best for page builds, bullets, layouts,
  variations, and bulk generation.
- **GPT** — versatile all-rounder. Great default for general copy, emails, and edits.
- **Grok** — punchy, current, bold. Best for hooks, edgy social angles, and snappy CTAs.
- **DeepSeek** — efficient and logical. Good for structured/technical or budget runs.

> No "wrong" choice — these are optimizations. If a model isn't available, use the
> closest match.
